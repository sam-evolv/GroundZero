## What this network means

Treat the export as a relationship graph and routing source, not as a mailing list. Connection status alone is weak evidence. Rank a route using:

1. Sam's direct knowledge of the relationship
2. current role and decision relevance, verified live when needed
3. shared history or credible context for contacting the person
4. company fit with the active commercial objective
5. whether the route can produce a buyer conversation, trusted introduction, funding access, or distribution

Do not infer warmth, authority or permission from a row in the export. Do not send outreach without Sam's approval.

7. Record material relationship corrections or outcomes as new provenance-backed events, never by silently rewriting this snapshot.
