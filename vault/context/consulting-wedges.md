# Consulting wedges to explore

## Principle
Do not sell "AI strategy" in the abstract.
Sell a fix for a painful workflow that already costs time, money, or reputation.

## Wedge 1: Residential developers
Problem:
- handover documentation is messy
- homeowner education is hard to prove
- aftercare gets lost across email, PDFs, and folders
- HPI / HPP evidence is awkward to assemble

Offer:
- AI-assisted handover and compliance evidence system
- document and training proof pack
- better aftercare tracking

## Wedge 2: Renewable installers / building services firms
Problem:
- commissioning docs, manuals, warranties, and homeowner training are scattered
- customers call with repetitive questions
- teams struggle to prove what was explained and when

Offer:
- AI-powered aftercare and homeowner support layer
- structured documentation and follow-up system
- searchable knowledge base for support teams

## Wedge 3: Solicitors / conveyancers
Problem:
- billable time and matter progress are hard to track cleanly
- case updates are repetitive
- admin steals focus from fee-earning work

Offer:
- AI workflow to capture matter updates, draft client comms, and reduce admin
- lightweight matter-status automation

## Wedge 4: Accountancy / compliance firms
Problem:
- client information is fragmented
- chasing documents is manual
- recurring reporting consumes time

Offer:
- AI document chase and workflow layer
- client intake and follow-up assistant
- recurring report builder

## Wedge 5: SMEs with recurring support and reporting
Problem:
- key data lives in email, PDFs, sheets, and staff memory
- decisions and follow-ups are not captured
- reporting is manually rebuilt every week or month

Offer:
- a shared operating layer for the company
- searchable internal knowledge
- automations for reporting and follow-up

## Best fit for a first consultancy offer
Start with a sector that already has:
- clear repetitive pain
- document-heavy workflows
- a budget for operational improvement
- short enough sales cycles to close quickly

Most likely first bets:
1. residential developers
2. renewable installers / building services
3. solicitors or conveyancers
