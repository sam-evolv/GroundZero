# Solo founder and agentic commerce thesis

## Core thesis
- AI lets one founder run a much higher-leverage business if the business is built around context engineering, simple stacks, and headless APIs.
- The best opportunities are usually narrow, high-value, rule-based workflows with clear budget and short sales cycles.
- The real moat is distribution, trust, and execution quality — not just model access.

## What to optimize for
- build in public early
- use boring infrastructure
- automate repeatable ops
- treat AI agents as staff with guardrails
- keep humans in control of customer-facing commitments

## What to avoid
- Medvi-style deceptive marketing or fake proof
- compliance shortcuts in regulated categories
- overbuilding before signal
- generic chatbot businesses with weak pricing power

## Best-fit opportunity types
- vertical AI agents
- agentic commerce interfaces
- headless B2B APIs wrapped in a lean distribution layer
- SMB and proptech workflows with recurring pain
- products where one operator can own the customer relationship and automate the back office
