# Irish food waste wedge

## Sector
Irish food manufacturing and processing, with an adjacent angle into grocery retail and food service.

## The bottleneck
Food waste across production, forecasting, inventory, shelf-life, and production planning.

This is a good wedge because it is:
- repetitive
- operationally visible
- tied to margin
- not as certification-heavy as healthcare
- large enough to justify a proper implementation budget

## Why it matters
The EPA says:
- Ireland generated about **835,000 tonnes** of food waste in 2023.
- Food waste costs the average Irish household about **€60 per month** or **€700 per year**.
- That implies an annual national cost of about **€1.29 billion**.
- In 2024, Irish manufacturing and processing generated about **305,000 tonnes** of food waste.

## Rough economic burden for manufacturing and processing
Using the EPA household benchmark as a transparent proxy:
- €1.29bn / 835,000 tonnes ≈ **€1,544.91 per tonne**
- 305,000 tonnes × €1,544.91 ≈ **€471.2 million per year**

Treat that as an estimated sector-equivalent burden, not an audited official figure.

## Product concept
**Yield & Waste Copilot**

An AI system that:
- predicts spoilage and excess production before it happens
- recommends batch sizes and reorder points
- flags slow-moving SKUs and near-expiry stock
- suggests alternate routing for byproducts and surplus stock
- auto-generates waste logs, donation paperwork, and QA notes
- learns from production history, demand signals, and seasonal patterns

## Best implementation wedge
Start with one narrow promise:
- reduce waste in one plant, line, or product family
- show margin recovered from overproduction, spoilage, and markdowns

## Delivery model
1. map waste hotspots from ERP / production / inventory data
2. build a live dashboard of avoidable waste
3. add forecasting and batch-sizing recommendations
4. add exception alerts and operator workflows
5. expand to multi-site performance management

## Plain-English pitch
"We help food manufacturers and processors cut waste and protect margin by predicting overproduction, managing shelf life, and turning messy production data into action."
