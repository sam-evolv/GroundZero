# OpenHouse sales routes and wedges

## Core positioning
OpenHouse is not just aftercare software. It is the evidence layer for handover quality, buyer education, compliance, and post-handover support.

## Strongest wedges

### 1. HPI / HPP compliance wedge
Use the Home Performance Index and Home Performance Pathway as a lead angle.

Why it works:
- developers want better sustainability credentials
- lenders and investors care about green certification
- the technical manual explicitly includes consumer information, aftercare, home user guides, and homeowner training
- OpenHouse can help prove the handover happened, not just say it happened

### 2. Finance wedge
HPI is already tied to green finance language and lender incentives.
OpenHouse can sit one layer above that by making the operational proof easy to capture and export.

### 3. Planning and ESG wedge
Developers, planning teams, sustainability consultants, and investors all need evidence.
OpenHouse can become the system that makes the project look more credible and lower risk.

### 4. Buyer experience wedge
Frame it as:
- better handover
- better education
- fewer support calls
- better buyer confidence
- less friction after completion

### 5. Aftercare / warranty wedge
The post-handover period is where trust is won or lost.
OpenHouse can become the aftercare log, evidence pack, and customer communication layer.

### 6. Energy assistant / low-carbon home OS wedge
Use heat pumps, solar PV, EV charging, smart-meter data, bills, tariffs, and device manuals as a new buyer-support and developer-evidence layer.

Why it works:
- new homes already contain more low-carbon infrastructure that buyers struggle to operate confidently
- HPI / HPP, green finance, ESG, and net-zero narratives all need better operational evidence
- developers can get anonymised scheme-level insight without turning OpenHouse into individual household surveillance
- the homeowner gets immediate value through bill explanations, tariff advice, solar self-consumption guidance, EV charging windows, and heat-pump confidence

See [[briefs/openhouse-energy-assistant-wedge]].

## Who to sell to first
Prioritise:
- property developers in Ireland
- property developers in the UK
- built-to-sell developers
- build-to-rent developers

Hold off on student accommodation for now.

## Lead sources to mine
- Irish Green Building Council member lists and HPI-related partners
- Home Performance Index and Home Performance Pathway project pages
- AIB / HBFI / green finance press releases
- planning applications for active residential schemes
- developer websites and LinkedIn
- architecture, quantity surveying, and sustainability consultants
- property development awards and sponsor lists
- HBF, IHBA, and UK developer networks

## Best outreach angles
1. "Help your scheme qualify more cleanly for HPI / HPP evidence and aftercare proof."
2. "Make buyer handover and training easier to prove."
3. "Reduce aftercare friction after completion."
4. "Create a better buyer experience and stronger ESG story."
5. "Turn handover documentation into a simple product, not a mess of PDFs."

## Practical next move
Build a target list of 50 developers and 20 partner channels, then test 3 message angles:
- compliance
- finance
- buyer experience
