# Irish prompt payment copilot

## Sector / beachhead
Irish project-based B2B firms, starting with **engineering consultancies** and similar technical service businesses.

Why this is a good beachhead:
- they already understand fee proposals, milestones, invoicing, and scope changes
- the sale is commercial, not clinical or heavily regulated
- the pain is recurring and cash-linked
- the tool can later expand into other B2B service sectors

## The bottleneck
**Late payments and manual collections admin**.

Typical pain points:
- chasing overdue invoices
- calculating and applying interest/charges correctly
- remembering which account needs a call, email, or escalation
- tracking partial payments and promises to pay
- matching invoices to POs / contracts / variations
- proving what was agreed when a client disputes payment

## Why it matters
Public sources say:
- Chambers Ireland estimated **almost €4 billion** could be owed to Irish businesses due to late payments, averaging **almost €23,000 per business**.
- The Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment says late payment interest is automatically payable in commercial transactions when invoices go unpaid under the statutory rules.
- The department also notes that in B2B contracts the agreed payment date cannot be grossly unfair to the supplier and, if greater than 60 days, must be expressly agreed.

## Product concept
**PromptPay Copilot**

A lightweight AI collections and payment-terms assistant that:
- reads invoices, contracts, POs, and emails
- spots overdue items automatically
- drafts polite but firm payment reminders
- calculates late-payment interest and compensation where applicable
- assembles dispute packs with supporting evidence
- logs every action for audit / handoff to finance
- escalates by account value, age, and likelihood of payment

## Why AI fits
This is ideal for AI because the work is:
- repetitive
- document-heavy
- rules-based
- language-heavy
- easy to human-review before sending

## Best implementation wedge
Do not start as a generic AR platform.

Start with one narrow promise:
- **"We recover overdue cash faster for Irish engineering consultancies."**

Phase 1:
- email ingestion
- invoice aging
- reminder drafting
- promise-to-pay tracking
- escalation queue

Phase 2:
- contract / PO matching
- variation / scope-change evidence capture
- interest calculations
- dispute pack generation

## Delivery model
Best as a **hybrid offer**:
- software tool + done-with-you setup
- optional ongoing collections ops / review service

That makes the first sale easier because the buyer is not just buying software; they are buying faster cash recovery.

## Plain-English pitch
"We help Irish engineering consultancies and similar B2B firms get paid faster by automating overdue invoice chasing, payment-term enforcement, and dispute evidence."
