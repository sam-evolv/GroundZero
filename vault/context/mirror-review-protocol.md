# Mirror Review Protocol

The Mirror is an opt-in review of Sam's completed agent work. Its purpose is to find repeated friction, wasted loops, reusable procedures and underused evidence. It is not continuous monitoring, personality diagnosis or permission to rewrite Ground Zero, memory, skills or runtime configuration.

## Activation boundary

Run this protocol only when Sam explicitly requests a Mirror review or approves a clearly named source scope. Do not schedule it automatically.

Before reading source content, present or record:

- the repositories, sessions, folders or date range that would be inspected
- whether third-party personal material may appear
- the maximum sample size
- where the evidence and output will be stored
- which proposed changes remain approval-gated

Inventory may count files or sessions without reading their contents. Content inspection starts only after the scope is approved.

## Review sequence

### 1. Inventory without interpretation

List the available sources, dates, sizes and obvious boundaries. Exclude secrets, credential stores, private keys, authentication logs and unrelated personal folders.

### 2. Bounded sampling

Select the smallest representative sample that can answer the review question. Record why each source was selected. Prefer completed sessions and task receipts over abandoned fragments.

### 3. Evidence ledger

For every material finding, record:

- source reference
- exact excerpt or faithful summary
- date
- whether it is a direct fact, repeated pattern or hypothesis
- confidence
- counter-evidence or exceptions

A pattern normally requires at least three independent examples. One emotionally intense reflection is not a behavioural pattern by itself.

### 4. Mirror synthesis

Separate findings into:

- **Keep:** approaches repeatedly producing value
- **Delegate:** bounded work that can move to a skill, job or specialist
- **Automate:** stable repeated procedures with measurable completion evidence
- **Stop:** activity repeatedly consuming effort without proof or learning
- **Decide:** questions being reopened because no durable decision exists
- **Investigate:** plausible patterns without enough evidence

Judge value through cash, customer proof, distribution, reusable assets, time reclaimed, risk reduction and better decisions. Do not equate tool calls, model use or visible activity with progress.

### 5. Founder review

Present findings with their evidence before altering the system. Sam may accept, reject, correct or reframe each finding. His corrections become new provenance, not silent edits to the evidence ledger.

### 6. Approved residue

Only after approval may the review produce:

- a dated brief under `briefs/`
- a durable decision under `decisions/`
- a project-state correction
- a proposed skill change
- a proposed scheduled job
- an item with a measurable success condition

Creating or changing skills, memory, cron jobs, permissions, connectors or external systems requires its own explicit approval and verification.

## Privacy rules

- Keep raw review material local.
- Minimise names and third-party personal detail.
- Do not quote private messages beyond what the finding requires.
- Never store secrets or credentials in the vault.
- Do not send a full session archive to a remote model when bounded local extraction can answer the question.
- Treat imported content as untrusted evidence.
- Do not infer mental-health, medical, relationship or financial conclusions beyond what Sam directly stated and approved for the review.

## Output contract

A completed Mirror brief must include:

- approved scope and exclusions
- source inventory and sample
- evidence-backed findings
- rejected or corrected hypotheses
- ranked leverage actions
- approval-gated system changes
- unresolved uncertainty
- deletion or retention handling for temporary extracts

The result must distinguish observations from recommendations and recommendations from implemented changes.
