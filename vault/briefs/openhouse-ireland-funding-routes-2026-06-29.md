# OpenHouse Ireland funding and backing routes

## Context

After Cairn declined as a usable large-developer route, OpenHouse needs funding, legitimacy, and channel access rather than relying only on founder-led enterprise sales into conservative developers.

## Recommended near-term target list

### 1. Enterprise Ireland Pre-Seed Start Fund — highest-priority public funding

- Link: https://www.enterprise-ireland.com/en/supports/pre-seed-start-fund
- Funding: **€50k or €100k convertible loan note**.
- Terms found: for each €50k tranche, Enterprise Ireland requires at least **€5k new cash co-investment** after application submission.
- Fit: very strong. OpenHouse has a live/beta-style product, early market learning, export potential, and can credibly frame as internationally traded software / home-energy intelligence.
- Action: prepare PSSF application now. Use the Cairn rejection as ICP refinement, not as a negative signal.

### 2. NDRC Accelerator — highest-priority accelerator/investor route

- Link: https://www.ndrc.ie/accelerator
- Funding: **€100k uncapped SAFE**, 20% discount, in two €50k tranches.
- Status: rolling applications; applications open.
- Fit: strong. NDRC backs globally scalable pre-seed tech companies with early proof points.
- Action: apply directly. Do not wait for Pre-Accelerator if the deck is good enough.

### 3. NDRC Pre-Accelerator — backup / narrative sharpening route

- Link: https://www.ndrc.ie/pre-accelerator
- Dates found:
  - Autumn batch: applications close **20 July 2026**; programme **24 Aug–1/2 Oct 2026**.
  - Winter batch: **27 Oct–4 Dec 2026**.
- Funding: no funding; free programme.
- Fit: useful if Sam wants structured validation, investor narrative, and warm ecosystem access before/alongside PSSF/NDRC Accelerator.
- Action: apply to Autumn as backup, while prioritising funded NDRC Accelerator and EI PSSF.

### 4. Enterprise Ireland New Frontiers Phase 2 — good non-dilutive support, time-costly

- Link: https://www.newfrontiers.ie/calendar
- Funding: **€15k tax-free stipend**, no equity.
- Relevant 2026 dates found:
  - SETU Carlow Phase 2: closes **7 July 2026**, starts **26 Aug 2026**.
  - Dundalk/DCU Phase 2: closes **19 July 2026**, starts September TBC.
  - ATU Galway/Mayo Phase 2: closes **23 July 2026**, starts October TBC.
  - TUS Limerick Phase 2: closes **4 Aug 2026**, starts **15 Sept 2026**.
  - TU Dublin Blanchardstown Phase 2: closes **5 Aug 2026**, starts October TBC.
  - Maynooth/TUS Athlone Phase 2: closes **28 Aug 2026**, starts **5 Oct 2026**.
- Fit: good if Sam can commit to a six-month full-time programme. Less ideal if speed to funding/customer pilots is urgent.
- Action: fallback or parallel application only.

### 5. Local Enterprise Office Priming Grant — possible, check eligibility and sequencing

- Link: https://www.localenterprise.ie/discover-business-supports/financial-supports/priming-grant/
- Funding: up to 50% of investment; usually max **€80k**, with **€150k** exceptional cases; micro-enterprises under 10 employees, generally within first 18 months.
- Fit: medium. Could support salaries, consultancy, marketing, overheads. If OpenHouse is EI HPSU-track/export SaaS, LEO may direct back to EI.
- Action: ask local LEO adviser whether OpenHouse can use Priming without complicating EI PSSF/HPSU path.

### 6. Enterprise Ireland HPSU / iHPSU / Feasibility route — next stage after PSSF

- HPSU Feasibility Grant found: max award **€30k** for investor-ready business plan.
- iHPSU: for High Potential Startups with MVP and international focus; often alongside private co-investment.
- Fit: strong later, but PSSF is likely the immediate EI instrument.
- Action: ask EI startup adviser which sequence is best: PSSF first vs HPSU Feasibility vs direct iHPSU later.

### 7. HBAN Angels — practical co-investment source for EI/PSSF

- Link: https://www.hban.org/
- Fit: strong. Useful for the required €5k+ PSSF co-investment and for market credibility.
- Action: apply to pitch. Target angels with property, construction, energy, SaaS, and climate backgrounds.

### 8. Furthr — EI/PSSF application support + VC later

- Link: https://www.furthr.ie/
- Fit: strong as navigation/support layer. Furthr supports EI PSSF and iHPSU applications; Furthr VC invests seed to Series A in Irish software/medtech; Furthr Foundry is a 12-week accelerator for pre-seed readiness.
- Action: contact Furthr specifically for PSSF readiness and ask whether OpenHouse is fit for Foundry or direct PSSF support.

### 9. Climate / energy-specific strategic routes

#### Accelerate Green / Bord na Móna
- Link: https://resolvepartners.ie/accelerate-green-2026-programme-now-open/
- Fit: medium-high if OpenHouse is framed around home energy intelligence, energy efficiency, grid/customer insight, and decarbonisation.
- Action: contact Resolve Partners / Accelerate Green to check if applications remain open or when the next cohort opens.

#### ESB Innovation / Free Electrons
- ESB Innovation: https://esb.ie/who-we-are/innovation
- Fit: strategic rather than immediate funding. ESB is relevant for energy systems, customer energy optimisation, and home-level intelligence.
- Action: prepare for next Free Electrons cycle; meanwhile approach ESB Eco Partners / innovation collaboration with a specific pilot concept.

#### CIRCULÉIRE Circular Venture Accelerator
- Link: https://www.circuleire.ie/accelerator
- Funding: €5k equity-free, six-month programme, late-stage circular ventures.
- Fit: weak-to-medium unless OpenHouse leans into circular homes/material lifecycle/digital building passports.
- Action: track 2027 only.

### 10. EU EIC Accelerator — ambitious, probably later

- Link: https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en
- Funding: grant below €2.5m; equity/quasi-equity €1m–€10m.
- 2026 full proposal batch dates: **8 July, 2 Sept, 4 Nov 2026** remaining. Short proposals can be submitted anytime and are batched monthly.
- Fit: later, unless OpenHouse can show TRL 6–8, defensible innovation, European scale, and market-creating potential.
- Action: 2027 target after pilots.

2. **NDRC / Dogpatch** — €100k SAFE + network.
3. **Furthr** — PSSF prep + Foundry + Furthr VC later.
4. **Enterprise Ireland startup team** — PSSF/HPSU sequencing.
5. **Resolve Partners / Accelerate Green** — climate/energy positioning.
6. **ESB Innovation / Eco Partners / Free Electrons** — strategic pilot route.
7. **Proptech Ireland** — property/developer ecosystem access, validation, and intros.
