# OpenHouse US regional-builder and channel prospect lane

## Use

The US lane is discovery, not a product-localisation project. Builders rank above channels when access is equal because one paid builder pilot is the goal. A credible channel can rank higher when it offers direct introductions to multiple suitable regional builders. Adjacent technology companies are partnership hypotheses and may be competitors.

## Builder targets

### 1. Garman Homes

- Geography: North Carolina Triangle and Triad markets.
- Verified operating owner: Richard Keebler, Division Warranty Manager. Garman also names additional warranty managers on its official warranty page.
- Why now: unusually visible warranty-team ownership, active communities and a direct contact route.
- Pilot angle: one community and one repeated plan type, exact-home record, grounded homeowner questions and cleaner escalation into the existing warranty team.
- Likely objection: the current warranty process and homeowner guidance are sufficient.
- Opening hook: “You already have unusually clear warranty ownership. OpenHouse would not replace it. I want to test whether a home-specific record resolves more information questions before they enter Richard's team and shows what evidence is repeatedly missing.”

  - https://garmanhomes.com/contact-us

### 2. Thrive Home Builders
