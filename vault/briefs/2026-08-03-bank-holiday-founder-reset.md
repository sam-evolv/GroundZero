## Candid read

Leaving the job before the January bonus would convert frustration into financial pressure without creating demand. Staying is not cowardice or failure. It is the rational default until one of the explicit exit gates in [[decisions/2026-08-03-day-job-bonus-and-openhouse-gates]] is met.

- Review the two ready-to-send messages only if useful. Do not continue polishing them indefinitely.

1. Send Carol the short acceptance message already prepared in [[items/oh-bridgewater-warm-introduction]].
