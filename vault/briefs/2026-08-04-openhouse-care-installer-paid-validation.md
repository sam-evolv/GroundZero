# OpenHouse Care installer paid-validation gate

## Verdict

**Hold product development. Go on one tightly bounded paid validation.**

The operational problem is credible, but willingness to pay is not proven. No further Care development cycle is justified until one installer pays for a pilot.

## Strongest wedge

**Post-installation issue intake, triage and resolution tracking across the installed base.**

The recurring pattern to test:

1. A homeowner reports low generation, an inverter warning, monitoring-login trouble, missing handover documents or an unresolved grant/export step.
2. The report arrives through phone, WhatsApp or email without the required context.
3. Office staff request address, system type, serial number, screenshots, error codes and photographs.
4. Someone checks the CRM or spreadsheet, manufacturer/monitoring portal, installation evidence and guidance.
5. The case becomes education, connectivity, paperwork, warranty, installer revisit or safety escalation.
6. Responsibility and resolution are tracked manually across several systems.

Care should therefore be positioned as a **structured renewable-aftercare front door and evidence trail**, not a generic homeowner chatbot or another field-service scheduler.

## Buyer

- Economic buyer: founder, Managing Director or Operations Director of an installer with a growing installed base.
- Operational champion: Service or Aftercare Manager.
- Daily users: office administrator, technical-support coordinator and field-service dispatcher.

Do not start with sole traders. Prioritise businesses with hundreds or thousands of completed installations, office/support staff, repeated homeowner calls, several manufacturer platforms and field engineers interrupted by basic triage.

## Current substitutes

Care would usually replace a fragmented workflow rather than one direct competitor:

- shared inbox, phone, WhatsApp and spreadsheets;
- manufacturer monitoring platforms such as FusionSolar or SolarEdge;
- OpenSolar for design, sales and customer project information;
- ServiceM8 and Commusoft for jobs, scheduling, forms, asset history and customer communication;
- generic CRM, ticketing or help-centre software.

The opening is renewable-specific aftercare orchestration across homeowner questions, installation records, monitoring evidence, warranties and escalation. If Care becomes job scheduling, ServiceM8 and Commusoft are already stronger.

## Pricing hypothesis

These are test values, not observed willingness to pay:

- 30-day concierge pilot: €500 payable before setup, covering up to 100 completed installations.
- Later setup: €1,000 to €2,500 for document templates, taxonomy, escalation rules and knowledge configuration.
- Later platform floor: €300 to €600 per installer per month.
- Potential volume expansion: approximately €1 to €3 per actively supported installation per month.

A €99 monthly tool is unlikely to cover onboarding and support. A five-figure annual contract is premature before Care proves fewer support hours, unnecessary call-outs, repeated contacts or time-to-resolution.

## Five-interview test

Interview exactly five companies before building:

- two founders or Managing Directors;
- two Service, Operations or Aftercare Managers;
- one office administrator who handles homeowner issues.

Ask for recent behaviour:

- Walk me through the last three post-installation problems.
- Where did each request arrive?
- What information was missing?
- Who touched the case?
- Which systems did they check?
- Did an engineer visit?
- How long did it remain open?
- What software are you already paying for?
- What happens after repeated homeowner calls or an SEAI complaint?
- Can you show the current process with customer details removed?

Interview pass: at least three independently describe the same intake/triage failure, and at least two quantify monthly case volume, staff time or avoidable site visits.

## Payment test

After interviews, offer one non-free concierge pilot:

- 30 days;
- no more than 100 existing installations;
- branded homeowner intake page;
- document, error-code and photograph collection;
- renewable-specific triage;
- human-approved responses;
- escalation queue and weekly outcome report;
- no integration beyond CSV and document upload;
- €500 payable before setup.

Commercial pass: one of five pays.

Strong pass: two pay, or one accepts a €1,000 setup plus at least €300 monthly continuation.

Compliments, interest, a free pilot or a non-binding letter do not count.
