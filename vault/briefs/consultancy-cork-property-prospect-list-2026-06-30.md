# Cork property consultancy prospect list

## Purpose

First concrete prospect list for the consultancy revenue sprint. This list is biased toward Cork property businesses where OpenHouse credibility is strongest and where a fixed-fee AI admin workflow could create visible value quickly.

## Offer to lead with

### AI Maintenance & Follow-Up Desk

**Promise:**

> Stop losing maintenance issues, viewing follow-ups and client updates in email, WhatsApp and photos.

**What gets implemented in 7–10 days:**

- enquiry / issue intake form
- email or WhatsApp-to-tracker workflow
- category and urgency tagging
- suggested replies
- contractor / client brief generator
- weekly landlord / vendor / developer report
- simple dashboard or spreadsheet tracker
- Loom training and handover SOP

**Pricing:**

- €500 paid audit, credited against build
- €3,500–€5,000 fixed implementation
- €750–€1,500/month support if useful

## Why this sector first

Property businesses are the closest consultancy wedge to OpenHouse. They already deal with messy documents, photos, support threads, maintenance issues, viewing enquiries and client reporting. The buyer can understand the pain without needing an AI education session.

The quick win I’m seeing is admin triage: turning property enquiries, maintenance emails, WhatsApps and photos into categorised items, suggested replies, contractor/client briefs and weekly client reports. I’m offering a small number of fixed-fee 10-day setups for local property businesses. Worth a 20-minute look at where your team is losing time?

## Shorter version for phone or LinkedIn

> I’m helping Cork property businesses clean up enquiry and maintenance admin with a fixed-fee 10-day AI workflow. No big software migration. It turns messy emails/photos/WhatsApps into tracked items, draft replies and weekly reports. Worth a quick look?

## Call script

### Opening

> I’m not selling AI training. I’m looking for one repetitive workflow that is costing your team time, then implementing a fixed workflow around it in 7–10 days.

### Questions

1. Where do new enquiries come in now: email, phone, WhatsApp, Daft/MyHome, website forms?
2. What gets missed or delayed most often?
3. Do you manage maintenance or just sales/lettings enquiries?
4. Are client/vendor/landlord updates manual?
5. Is there one weekly report or update that someone hates preparing?
6. If I could remove one admin bottleneck in 10 days, what should it be?

### Close

> The paid audit is €500. I map the workflow, identify the fastest win, and give you a fixed implementation quote. If you go ahead, the audit is credited against the build.
