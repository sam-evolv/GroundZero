# Evolv NDMG grant-uplift transition gate

## Bounded proposal

Add one temporary **NDMG grant-uplift transition receipt** to the existing [[briefs/2026-09-23-evolv-commercial-solar-grant-grid-readiness-receipt]] only if final SEAI implementation documents are published and Evolv has a real, permissioned commercial rooftop opportunity whose purchase or works have not started.

For that one opportunity, the receipt would record:

- the application's exact stage and dated governing documents;
- the existing grant offer, if any, without cancelling or changing it;
- the final published uplift window, grant basis, cap or bands and eligible-cost definition;
- the official treatment of applications and offers made before the new window;
- the quote, purchase, works and offer-expiry dates that make delay consequential;
- a source-linked old-versus-new grant bridge using only the final rules; and
- one controlled result: `CURRENT OFFER GOVERNS ON RECORDED EVIDENCE`, `POTENTIAL UPLIFT — SEAI CONFIRMATION REQUIRED`, or `NO UPLIFT ESTABLISHED`.

This is not a grant application, cancellation or reapplication service, financial or grant advice, a savings promise, a quote change, or a reason to delay a project. The commercial question is narrower: **can a dated transition receipt prevent one owner from relying on the wrong grant amount or making an unsupported timing decision during a short policy window?**

## Why this is genuinely new but subordinate

The existing grant-and-grid receipt was researched before the 29 September announcement and tests the ordinary pre-commit evidence path. [[briefs/2026-09-29-evolv-commercial-solar-triple-e-aca-decision-receipt]] uses the grant-net cost as an accountant-confirmed input but does not address a temporary grant-rate transition.

The new evidence introduces a distinct, time-bounded question: the Government and SEAI announced a proposed nine-month NDMG increase from about 23% to 45% of eligible installation costs, intended to open on 6 October 2026, while the live scheme page and Version 1.4 Application Guide still show the existing fixed kWp schedule at the 2 October research cutoff. The announcement also says final implementation arrangements and full terms are still due.

This is therefore a **wait-for-authority and compare** gate, not a new product lane. If useful, merge the tested fields into the existing grant-and-grid receipt. Do not create a grant consultancy, calculator or portal. The July-recorded Renew survey state is stale, so this note does not assert that a qualifying live opportunity exists.

## Verified evidence

- The official SEAI and Government announcement is dated 29 September 2026. It says the measures are intended to open on 6 October 2026, subject to final implementation arrangements, and that full terms, conditions and eligibility details will be available in due course.
- The announcement's business-measures table describes a temporary NDMG increase to a proposed 45% of eligible costs. Its footnote says the scheme currently provides fixed support per installed capacity and proposes increasing support from about 23% of installation cost to 45% for nine months.
- The same announcement says NDMG has supported 3,160 installations and received more than 6,100 applications since 2022. That indicates scheme use, not Evolv demand or availability of funding for a particular applicant.
- At the 2 October cutoff, SEAI's live NDMG page still publishes the existing capacity-based schedule: €900/kWp for the first 2 kWp, the stated intermediate bands, €300/kWp from 7–20 kWp, €200/kWp from 21–200 kWp, €150/kWp from 201–1,000 kWp, and a displayed maximum of €162,600. It does not yet publish a 45% calculation method or transition rule.
- The current linked Application Guide identifies itself as Version 1.4 dated 16 July 2026. It repeats the fixed capacity bands and maximum, requires approval before purchase or works, limits support to one system per MPRN, and gives an approved applicant eight months to complete works and submit the required evidence.
- The current terms say the application-date guide, form and terms apply, while allowing SEAI to revise or supplement terms after submission unless the applicant withdraws. They do not say that an existing application or accepted offer automatically receives the announced uplift.
- The terms also say funding is limited, payment is first come, first served once conditions are met, and payment may be deferred if the relevant year's funding is exhausted. This does not prove that the nine-month uplift budget will be exhausted or that an applicant should rush.

The evidence establishes a material policy transition and a current source conflict. It does **not** establish the final grant bands or cap, the exact nine-month start and end dates, what costs qualify, how pre-6 October applications or offers will be treated, whether the existing premises-age rule changes, or that any Evolv opportunity is eligible.

## Assumptions to falsify

- Evolv has a current, permissioned commercial rooftop opportunity that remains before purchase and works.
- The final implementation documents materially change that opportunity's grant amount or timing.
- The application's stage and existing offer can be established without cancelling, editing or resubmitting anything.
- The registered installer or ordinary SEAI process does not already provide a clear, dated transition explanation.
- A short wait for one authoritative fact does not jeopardise the quote, grid route, project programme, existing grant offer or eight-month completion period.
- A source-linked comparison changes a real owner decision instead of merely repeating the announcement.

## Smallest validation test

### Gate 0 — wait for final authority

Before using any customer record, wait for SEAI to publish the final post-announcement scheme page, Application Guide, terms, grant calculator or formal transition notice. Freeze the pre-change and final sources by title, visible version or date, URL and retrieval date. Compare only published fields; do not infer the rate schedule from the press release.

If the final documents do not define treatment of pre-existing applications or offers, record that as unresolved. Do not cancel, withdraw, reapply, start works, delay works or contact SEAI from this note.

### Gate 1 — one permissioned transition receipt

If a suitable current opportunity exists after Gate 0:

1. Obtain Sam's approval for the exact opportunity and the conflict, confidentiality and IP boundary, then obtain the registered customer's approval for the minimum records and purpose.
2. In an approved client workspace, capture only a controlled site ID, system kWp, application date, application stage, offer date and amount, offer expiry, quote date, purchase or works status, and the final scheme-source versions. Keep the MPRN, customer identity, credentials, full quote and application files out of Ground Zero.
3. Classify the stage as `NOT APPLIED`, `SUBMITTED`, `OFFER ISSUED`, `OFFER ACCEPTED`, `PURCHASE COMMITTED`, `WORKS STARTED` or `COMPLETED`, with a source for the classification.
4. Calculate an old-versus-new bridge only from final published rules and the exact eligible-cost definition. Keep VAT, grant-aid interaction, excluded lines and unknowns separate. Do not use the headline 45% as a customer-specific grant amount unless the final documents support it.
5. Preserve the existing offer and all deadlines. If transition treatment remains ambiguous, seek separate approval before asking SEAI or the registered installer for written confirmation through the normal project channel.
6. Compare the result with the ordinary quote and the existing grant-and-grid receipt. Record whether it prevents an unsupported delay, exposes a material grant assumption or changes a real pre-commit decision.
7. Return one outcome: **merge the transition fields into the existing manual receipt**, **monitor one named missing authority**, or **discard**. Do not build software or market the uplift from one case.

### Pass gate

Retain the transition fields only if:

- the final rules and treatment of the exact application stage are source-linked or visibly unresolved;
- the comparison prevents an unsupported timing action or exposes a material net-cost assumption;
- preparation takes no more than 30 additional minutes after approved records are available;
- the registered installer, SEAI or another competent authority confirms any ambiguous transition treatment before reliance; and
- the owner or delivery lead says the receipt materially improves a live decision.

A larger headline grant, generic urgency or a polished comparison with no changed decision does not pass.

### Kill gate

Discard or park the wedge if there is no suitable current opportunity, purchase or works have already made the transition irrelevant, the final rules apply the change automatically and clearly, the installer already provides an equivalent receipt, the grant difference is immaterial, the treatment cannot be confirmed before a real deadline, or delay risk exceeds the potential value.

## Downside and safeguards

- **Premature reliance:** the 29 September announcement is explicit that final implementation arrangements and full terms are still due. Do not quote a customer-specific 45% grant from the announcement alone.
- **Transition error:** an application, submitted application, issued offer and accepted offer may be treated differently. Preserve the exact stage and do not infer automatic uplift.
- **Delay risk:** waiting can affect quote validity, connection sequencing, the eight-month grant window and installation programme. This note does not recommend waiting or accelerating.
- **Withdrawal risk:** the current terms mention withdrawal when terms change, but do not promise that withdrawal and reapplication preserves eligibility, priority, budget or grant value. Do not cancel or reapply without authoritative confirmation and explicit approval.
- **Eligible-cost and cap risk:** 45% of eligible costs is not necessarily 45% of the contract price. Final bands, caps, VAT treatment, excluded lines and evidence requirements remain open.
- **Grant interaction:** keep the new NDMG amount separate from the accountant-confirmed ACA basis and the prohibition on cumulative Irish grant-aid. Do not promise stacked support.
- **Funding risk:** limited funding and first-come payment language do not justify artificial urgency or a guaranteed award claim.
- **Commercial sensitivity:** quotations, application records and financing details are confidential. Minimise fields and keep customer records outside Ground Zero.
- **Priority drift:** this temporary check remains subordinate to the real rooftop sale, [[items/renew-reporting-source-baseline]] and the paid 60–90 day owner-side baseline in [[project_state/renew]].

## Approval boundary

This note authorises only public-source research and proposes one later, static, permissioned receipt. It does not authorise:

- customer, installer, SEAI, accountant, lender, supplier or third-party contact;
- access to a grant portal, MPRN, customer identity, quote, offer, invoice, tax record, credentials or client file;
- applying, withdrawing, cancelling, editing, resubmitting or accepting a grant application or offer;
- delaying or accelerating purchase, works, connection, commissioning or payment;
- a quote change, grant calculation for reliance, financing commitment, tax position, contract or works instruction;
- advertising the uplift, publishing savings, outreach, spending, software development, automation or production mutation.

Sam must first approve the exact opportunity and evidence scope after conflict, confidentiality and IP review. The registered customer must approve the records and purpose. SEAI or the registered installer must confirm unresolved scheme-transition treatment, and the customer's accountant must confirm any tax or grant-net implications. Any contact, application action, quote change, paid pilot or second-site test requires separate approval.
