# OpenHouse one-scheme paid pilot proposal template

Use this only after a qualified discovery conversation. Replace every bracketed field with facts learned from the buyer. Keep the final proposal to one or two pages.

## OpenHouse pilot for [Developer] at [Scheme or phase]

### Why this pilot

[Developer] currently handles [handover, home information, homeowner questions, warranty or aftercare workflow] through [current process or systems]. During discovery, the team identified [specific repeated work, evidence gap, customer friction or management blind spot].

The pilot will test whether one exact-home record and a grounded homeowner-guidance layer can improve that workflow without replacing [Developer]'s existing CRM, warranty, document or contractor systems.

### Scope

- Development or phase: [name]
- Homes: [20 to 100]
- Developer owner: [name and role]
- OpenHouse owner: Sam Donworth
- Test-home validation: [date]
- Homeowner launch: [date or condition]
- Measurement period: [six weeks from receipt of approved inputs]
- Decision meeting: [date]

### Developer inputs

[Developer] will provide:

- approved unit list and home identifiers;
- approved handover documents, manuals, warranties and scheme information;
- designated operational owner;
- escalation contact for questions OpenHouse cannot answer from approved evidence;
- agreed homeowner invitation route;
- written approval of the validated test home before wider activation.

The delivery period starts when the agreed inputs are complete and usable.

### OpenHouse delivery

OpenHouse will:

- create one controlled record for each approved home;
- organise approved documents, systems, warranties and history by exact home;
- provide secure homeowner access;
- answer homeowner questions only from the approved home record and defined support rules;
- escalate unsupported questions to the agreed developer route;
- record question categories, repeated evidence gaps and escalation demand;
- provide a weekly operating summary;
- provide a final pilot review and rollout recommendation.

### Measures

Select three to five measures that match the discovered problem:

- percentage of invited homes activated;
- percentage of activated homes asking at least one question;
- number and categories of property-specific questions;
- percentage answered from approved evidence;
- repeated missing-information themes;
- number of questions requiring developer escalation;
- time required to prepare and validate each home;
- customer-care assessment of record usefulness;
- agreed continue, change or stop decision.

Do not include a metric unless both parties can actually observe it.

### Commercial terms

Working structure:

- scheme setup: €[amount];
- per provisioned home: €[amount];
- pilot cohort: [number] homes;
- total pilot fee: €[amount];
- payment: [for example, 50% on signature and 50% on validated cohort launch];
- taxes: [VAT treatment];
- proposal validity: [date].

Internal starting hypothesis only:

- €2,500 setup;
- €99 per provisioned home;
- minimum practical pilot value of approximately €5,000.

Adapt the currency and price to the buyer, scope, support burden and procurement context. Sam approves every external number.

### Responsibilities and boundaries

- OpenHouse uses only agreed developer evidence and defined support rules.
- The developer remains responsible for defects, warranty decisions, contractor instruction, legal compliance and safety-critical advice.
- OpenHouse will not make autonomous warranty-coverage decisions.
- Unsupported, ambiguous or safety-critical questions are escalated.
- Production access, data processing and homeowner launch remain subject to the agreed security and privacy gates.

### Exclusions

Unless added in writing, the pilot excludes:

- replacement of the developer's CRM, ERP, defect or warranty platform;
- custom integrations;
- portfolio-wide migration;
- contractor dispatch;
- legal or warranty-coverage determinations;
- device control;
- measured savings or workload-reduction guarantees;
- product features not required by the approved pilot workflow.

### Decision

At the decision meeting, [Developer] and OpenHouse will choose one of:

1. extend the pilot with defined changes;
2. proceed to a paid scheme or portfolio rollout;
3. stop and document the reason.

A continuation decision must name the commercial owner, delivery scope, price and next date.

- The pilot is paid.
