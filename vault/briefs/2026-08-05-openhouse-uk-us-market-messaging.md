# OpenHouse UK and US market messaging

## Constant product truth

OpenHouse creates one exact-home record for documents, systems, warranties and history. Homeowners receive guidance grounded in approved information for their home. The developer or builder can see repeated questions, missing information and where human follow-up is required.

The market language changes. The core product and evidence standard do not.

## UK message

### Buyer problem

A UK builder must coordinate sales information, completion, handover, after-sales support and complaints while preserving a usable record of what the customer received and what happened afterward.

The New Homes Quality Code Version 2 applies to homes reserved from 2 March 2026 and covers the process from marketing and sales through after-sales service. Customers of registered developers can access the independent New Homes Ombudsman Service where Code requirements are not met.

Sources checked 5 August 2026:

- https://www.nhqb.org.uk/
- https://www.nhqb.org.uk/the-code/
- https://www.nhqb.org.uk/register-of-developers/

### Positioning

> OpenHouse is the exact-home record and homeowner-guidance layer between handover information and after-sales operations. It does not replace the CRM or defect platform. It makes the home-specific evidence usable to the customer and reveals what the existing handover still fails to answer.

### First wedge

- one scheme or phase;
- 20 to 50 plots;
- one validated home before wider launch;
- home-specific handover record;
- grounded homeowner questions;
- repeated evidence-gap report;
- escalation into the existing customer-care workflow;
- measured continue, change or stop decision.

### Language to use

- plots;
- completions;
- handover information;
- homeowner orientation or settling in;
- customer care and after-sales;
- snagging and defects only where the buyer uses those terms;
- New Homes Quality Code-aligned evidence support.

### Language to avoid

- “Ombudsman-ready” unless independently reviewed against the precise workflow;
- replacing Clixifix, Spaciable, CRM or defect software;
- legal compliance automation;
- guaranteed complaint reduction;
- autonomous defect or warranty decisions.

### Competitor response

If the buyer uses Spaciable, Clixifix or another portal:

> That may already handle the workflow you need. I am not proposing a replacement. The question is whether the individual-home information is actually usable to the homeowner, whether their questions are answered from approved evidence, and whether your team can see what information is repeatedly missing. If the existing system already does that well, there may be no gap for OpenHouse.

## US message

### Buyer problem

A regional US builder coordinates homeowner handoff, home-specific information, warranty intake, trade follow-up and quality learning across communities and house plans. The exact workflow and legal requirements vary by state and by warranty structure.

### Positioning

> OpenHouse is the exact-home record and homeowner-guidance layer that sits alongside the builder's warranty and ERP systems. It helps homeowners use the documents, systems and warranties that belong to their home, and gives the builder cleaner visibility into repeated questions and missing information before those become unnecessary warranty traffic.

### First wedge

- one community or phase;
- 20 to 50 recently completed or soon-to-close homes;
- one repeated plan or controlled home cohort where practical;
- exact-home documents, systems and warranty record;
- grounded homeowner questions;
- unsupported questions routed into the current warranty process;
- repeated question and evidence-gap report;
- measured continue, change or stop decision.

### Language to use

- homebuilder;
- community;
- closing and homeowner handoff;
- homeowner orientation;
- warranty intake and homeowner care;
- home-specific guide;
- coexistence with current warranty and ERP systems.

### Language to avoid

- warranty coverage decisions;
- right-to-repair compliance claims;
- replacing the builder's ERP or warranty administrator;
- automatic trade dispatch;
- guaranteed call or truck-roll reduction;
- US readiness claims involving SOC 2, data residency, SMS or integrations that have not been delivered.

### Incumbent response

If the builder uses AvidWarranty, Punchlist Manager, 2-10, RWC, StrucSure or another system:

> I am not asking you to replace the warranty system of record. OpenHouse is testing whether the homeowner can resolve more home-specific questions from approved information, and whether your team gets a cleaner record of what information was available, what was asked and what still required warranty intervention.

## Founder credibility

Use Sam's operator background early:

> I am one of three people running a residential developer in Ireland. I built OpenHouse from the handover and aftercare workflow itself, not from an abstract software thesis.

Then move directly to the buyer's process. Do not make the call a biography.

## Proof line

> OpenHouse is already operating across four developments internally. In the conservative evidence set, homeowners generated 259 property-specific questions across 79 home contexts, including 245 questions typed in their own words. I am now looking for one independent developer or homebuilder to test a defined paid pilot.

This proves usage and repeated information demand. It does not prove external willingness to pay, measured savings or answer accuracy.
