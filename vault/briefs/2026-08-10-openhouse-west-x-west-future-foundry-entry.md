# OpenHouse West X West Future Foundry entry

## Decision

OpenHouse is an excellent category and founder fit for the West X West Future Foundry, but it is not eligible under the published incorporation rule as currently written.

The official page requires the applicant company to have been registered in Ireland for at least 12 months. OpenHouse Ai Limited was incorporated on 4 February 2026. At the 4 September 2026 deadline it will have been incorporated for 212 days, approximately seven months, and will still be 153 days short of its first anniversary.

Do not submit while representing OpenHouse as meeting the 12-month requirement. First email the organiser for a written eligibility decision. The programme explicitly invites uncertain applicants to contact `info@westxwest.ie` before the deadline.
