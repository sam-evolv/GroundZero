# OpenHouse UK/US expansion research

## Ranked expansion thesis

1. **UK new-build warranty / compliance aftercare wedge — best near-term fit.** UK has a concentrated, regulation-driven new-build quality ecosystem. NHBC says its warranty/insurance policies protect about **1.2m UK homes**. The New Homes Quality Code requires registered developers to provide quality, fairness and good customer service through buying, moving and settling in, with Ombudsman access for two years post-completion. Sell OpenHouse as an AI-first homeowner evidence + aftercare layer: photo-to-issue capture, snagging triage, NHQB/Ombudsman-ready evidence packs, home identity/manual and energy/performance guidance. Target SME/mid-market builders, housing associations and BTR operators before top-tier enterprise builders.

2. **US new-home builder warranty operations wedge — larger market, more fragmented.** US Census reported May 2026 new single-family sales at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of **580k** and **496k** new houses for sale. Builder warranty is a standard buyer-confidence/risk-management motion. Sell an AI homeowner warranty concierge for regional production builders: photo/video-to-warranty request, coverage triage, trade routing, homeowner updates and quality analytics. Start with builders in Texas, Florida, Carolinas, Arizona and Colorado; later partner with warranty administrators.

3. **UK retrofit / green mortgage / insurer home-performance passport — strategic but slower.** Use the home identity to become a Home Performance Passport: verified asset record, improvement plan, evidence of measures, grant/finance guidance and lender/insurer-ready documentation. Start as an add-on to new-build handover; partner for existing-home retrofit.

4. **US utility/rebate/electrification concierge — big incentives, hardest channel.** ENERGY STAR says homeowners can save up to **$3,200** in federal tax credits through 2025, including 30% credits up to $2,000 for heat-pump technology and 30% up to $1,200 for other efficiency measures; DOE rebates are state-administered. Best route is builder/HOA/lender aggregation, not direct competition with utility platforms.

5. **HOA/community management — possible distribution, weaker product fit.** Use as a channel for master-planned communities, warranty and energy adoption; avoid competing head-on with full HOA management systems unless product expands to ARC requests, violations, payments and board workflows.

## Competitor / adjacent landscape

### UK new-build / aftercare
- **Spaciable** — property management portal for homebuilders; post-completion, customer care, home user guides, languages, NHQB compliance. Claims 42% less handover/admin time, 85% less compliance admin and 75% faster resident issue response.
- **Clixifix** — defect/customer-care software for housebuilders, housing associations and contractors; defect/repair management, contractor portals, resident portal, complaints module, configurable forms, defect-cost analysis, reports. Claims 320k+ defects resolved in 2025, 109k+ plots added and 25k+ users added.
- **PlanRadar** — construction and real-estate management across build and operations.
- **Fixflo / Plentific** — repairs, maintenance and property operations, strong in lettings, BTR, block/social housing.
- **Zutec** — digital handover/building information/logbook adjacency.

### US builder warranty / homeowner handover
- **Verisk Punchlist Manager** — end-to-end homebuilder warranty management; tracks/resolves/communicates warranty items; integrates warranty, service and QA; homeowner/trade communication, portal, automated notifications and quality trend analytics.
- **2-10 Home Buyers Warranty** — builder warranty administrator; warranties cover workmanship, distribution systems and structural components; reduces builder risk and increases buyer confidence.
- **RWC Warranty** — written insured new-home warranties/risk management; over 4m homes warranted since 1981.
- **StrucSure** — builder/contractor and homeowner portals, service request/claim workflow, 1-2-10 warranty, First Call Warranty Service.
- **Buildertrend, Hyphen BuildTopia, ECI MarkSystems, Constellation HomeBuilder Systems, ProHome, Quality Built** — broader builder ERP/customer care/warranty ecosystem.
- **HomeBinder / Centriq** — home manual/home records adjacency.

### Home energy / retrofit / utility engagement
- **Uplight** — utility clean energy platform: efficiency/electrification journeys, marketplaces, energy reports, customer portals, demand response/DER/VPP.
- **Bidgely** — utility AI energy analytics: disaggregation, customer engagement, DSM, load flexibility, electrification, affordability targeting.
- **Sense** — home/utility real-time energy intelligence; claims 4-8% average annual energy savings, 3x demand savings vs behavioural programs and 30x portal engagement.
- **Eliq** — utility customer energy insights and operational intelligence.
- **Snugg** — UK personalised home energy efficiency plan, grants, installers and financing.
- **Sero** — UK social-housing retrofit/funding/smart energy services.
- **EnergySage / Zero Homes** — US clean home energy marketplace and managed heat-pump/electrification journeys.

## Likely buyer/customer paths

### UK aftercare path
- **Economic buyer:** COO, Customer Care/Aftercare Director, Quality Director, Technical Director.
- **Influencers:** NHQB/New Homes Ombudsman compliance owners, warranty provider contacts, site/customer-care teams, HBF survey/reputation owners.
- **Pilot:** One development or 100-300 completions for 90 days.
- **Metrics:** admin hours saved, first-response time, issue resolution time, evidence completeness, fewer escalations, recurring defects by trade/plot, homeowner satisfaction.

### US warranty path
- **Economic buyer:** VP Warranty/Customer Experience/Quality at regional production builder.
- **Influencers:** warranty administrators, risk/legal, trade coordinator, construction ops, IT.
- **Pilot:** warranty intake + evidence module that coexists with ERP/warranty systems.
- **Metrics:** fewer incomplete requests, faster triage, lower repeat visits, cleaner dispute evidence, improved referral/customer satisfaction.

### Energy/finance path
- **Economic buyer:** green mortgage lead, insurer proposition owner, utility DSM/electrification lead, housing association retrofit lead.
- **Customer:** homeowner uses OpenHouse as a passport/concierge; channel partner monetizes lower risk, funded works or qualified leads.
- **Pilot:** new-build homeowner annual energy review and verified improvement/evidence pack.

## Product changes required

### UK
- UK terminology: plots, reservations, completions, handover packs, snagging, NHQB, New Homes Ombudsman, NHBC Buildmark, LABC/Premier Guarantee.
- GDPR/UK hosting/security positioning.
- Exportable evidence packs: timestamps, photos, correspondence, chronology, contractor assignment, sign-off, warranty status.
- Dashboard: SLA aging, recurring defects, trade/plot analytics, Ombudsman risk flags, HBF/NHQB survey links.
- Integrations/coexistence with Spaciable/Clixifix/ERP/document libraries.

### US
- 1-2-10 warranty periods, state-specific warranty/right-to-repair logic, arbitration/dispute evidence.
- SMS-heavy homeowner comms, scheduling/trade routing, builder ERP integrations.
- SOC 2, US data residency, SSO/RBAC.
- Risk analytics by community/plan/subcontractor, severity prediction and claims documentation.

### Energy / passport
- EPC/SAP/RdSAP ingestion for UK; US Home Energy Score/utility data/Green Button where available.
- Incentive/rebate eligibility, installer/referral workflow, tax/finance disclaimers.
- Evidence packs for lenders/insurers: photos, invoices, certificates, warranties, EPC/energy delta, savings/carbon estimates.
- Device/data integrations: smart thermostats, solar/battery, EVSE, heat pumps, smart meter/panel capacity.

## Recommended next steps

1. Choose **UK aftercare/compliance** as primary beachhead and **US builder warranty** as secondary discovery track.
2. Build a UK demo: NHQB-coded evidence workflow, homeowner snagging app, developer dashboard, warranty-provider export and sample Ombudsman-ready case file.
3. Create battlecards: Spaciable = portal/handover/compliance; Clixifix = defect workflow; OpenHouse = AI home model + evidence + homeowner engagement + energy lifecycle.
4. Offer 90-day regional-builder pilot priced per completion/plot plus platform.
5. Interview 10 US regional builder warranty leaders and 3 warranty administrators before building US-specific workflow depth.
6. Add lightweight energy/passport fields now; defer full rebate/utility engine until a channel partner commits.
