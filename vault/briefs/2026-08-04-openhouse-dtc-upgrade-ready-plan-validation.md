# OpenHouse DTC €79 Upgrade Ready Plan validation

## Decision

Do not validate a €5 monthly subscription now. Validate a €79 decision product first.

**Offer:** OpenHouse Upgrade Ready Plan

> Upload your BER or BER advisory report. In ten minutes, see the first verified Money, Comfort and Risk signals in your home. Pay €79 only if you want the complete evidence-backed plan and Home Record starter.

The free reveal tests activation and trust. A retained €79 payment tests willingness to pay. Later behaviour tests whether a persistent Home Record creates recurring value.

## Why this wedge

- Chimni offers a free homeowner digital logbook with document storage, dashboard and planning functions. Organised home data alone therefore has a free substitute.
- Ireland's HomeDLBX positions handover documents, warranties, asset registers, maintenance records and lifecycle updates as a digital home record, but does not publicly validate direct consumer subscription pricing.
- Irish homeowners already pay for decision and outcome products. Public pricing reviewed on 4 August includes roughly €180 to €300 for common HomeSnag inspection configurations and €135.20 to €159.20 for discounted annual Bord Gáis Energy boiler service/cover options.
- UK HomeServe prices monthly boiler/heating cover from £6.99 to £11.99, but those products include repairs and risk transfer. They do not validate a software-only subscription.
- AIB's 2024 consumer research reports strong interest in energy improvement and identifies affordability as the leading barrier. The useful commercial job is therefore reducing uncertainty about what to do first, not simply cataloguing the home.

## Free Ten-Minute Home Reveal

### Inputs

- Confirmed address or Eircode
- BER or advisory report
- One goal: lower bills, improve comfort, plan an upgrade, or understand what to fix first

### Output

- Three sourced facts from the evidence
- One Money signal
- One Comfort signal
- One Risk or uncertainty signal
- One missing fact that could materially change the recommendation
- Preview of the proposed Home Record

No card and no full-home questionnaire.

## €79 paid plan

### Promise

> Get your complete Upgrade Ready Plan for €79, delivered within 72 hours. If OpenHouse cannot identify one evidence-backed action, professional question or evidence gap that advances the homeowner's decision, refund the €79.

This is a useful-action guarantee, not a savings guarantee.

### Paid inputs

- BER or advisory report
- Latest electricity or heating bill
- Up to five guided photos of relevant fabric, heating system and controls
- Existing contractor quote when applicable

### Output

- Sourced Home Record starter separating known facts, assumptions and gaps
- Ranked 90-day Money, Comfort and Risk plan
- Do now, investigate and defer classification
- Questions for a BER assessor, contractor or installer
- Quote dependencies and ordering risks
- Document, warranty and maintenance index for supplied evidence
- One next-best evidence request
- One asynchronous clarification and one revision

### Boundary

Include desktop research, evidence interpretation, a bounded supplied-evidence Home Record, one revision and delivery within 72 hours.

Exclude site visits, new BERs, technical surveys, engineering/electrical/financial advice, definitive defect diagnosis, contractor selection, savings guarantees, smart-device connections, ongoing monitoring, unlimited chat and full-home inventory completion.

Target median founder fulfilment after the first ten plans: no more than 90 minutes.

## Acquisition test

One Ireland-only paid-search surface around active upgrade intent, for example understanding a BER report or deciding which home upgrade to do first.

Landing proposition:

> Your BER gives you a rating. OpenHouse shows you what to do next. Upload it and get your first home-specific Money, Comfort and Risk reveal in ten minutes.

Do not mix UK, moving-home, snagging or generic home-management audiences into this first cohort.

## Validation gates

Run until 100 valid reveals or 20 paid plans.

### Pass

- 15 or more retained €79 purchases per 100 completed reveals
- At least 20% of qualified landing visitors complete a valid reveal
- Paid CAC at or below €25
- At least 80% rate usefulness 4/5 or better
- Refund rate at or below 10%
- At least 60% voluntarily provide a second evidence item
- At least 50% take or schedule a recommended action within 30 days
- Median fulfilment at or below 90 minutes
- At least three finding patterns repeat across unrelated homes

### Iterate once

- 8 to 14 retained purchases per 100 reveals
- Strong usefulness but weak payment conversion
- One dominant objection that can be tested by changing only the promise, proof or input requirement

### Fail or reframe

- Fewer than 8 retained purchases per 100 reveals
- Refund rate above 20%
- Fewer than 30% provide another evidence item or act
- Median fulfilment remains above 150 minutes after ten deliveries
- Paid CAC above €40
- Users say a generic LLM upload was sufficient

## Subscription gate

After at least 30 retained €79 customers, observe 90-day behaviour without manufacturing reasons to return. Test a manual €59 annual membership only if:

- at least 40% return for a second real home job without reacquisition;
- at least 30% experience two distinct non-chat value events;
- at least 25%, eight of 30, actually pay €59 annually;
- recurring support and variable costs preserve at least 70% gross margin;
- value comes from the persistent Home Record, not repeated generic questions.

Even then, an annual manual pilot precedes subscription infrastructure. Twelve-month renewal is the actual recurring-revenue proof.
