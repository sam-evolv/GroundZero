## Product thesis

OpenHouse captures construction-stage context before it disappears and creates a living model of the home. That model supports practical, home-specific homeowner answers with visible sources. It can expand into energy intelligence framed through Money, Comfort and Risk. At scheme level, developers can see what OpenHouse resolves, what needs a person and what knowledge gaps recur.

The current strategic creative idea is:

> A home with a memory.

Potential opening line:

> A home that can explain itself.

Do not accept these blindly. Test them against the product and improve them if a stronger, clearer and more ownable expression exists.

## Mandatory memory hooks

The final experience must make a visitor remember:

1. The best context exists while the home is being built.

3. OpenHouse gives home-specific answers that can be checked against their source.
4. Energy is translated into Money, Comfort and Risk.
5. One home’s questions become scheme-level developer insight.
