# Bridgewater Developments · OpenHouse discovery brief

> Superseded for the active meeting by [[briefs/2026-08-04-bridgewater-meeting-preparation]]. This note remains as the pre-introduction research snapshot.

## Objective

Earn a useful second conversation or a small, defined pilot by understanding Bridgewater's real handover and post-completion workflow. Do not pitch a generic portal or attempt to diagnose their operations from their website.

## Publicly verified context

Bridgewater Group / Bridgewater Construction / Bridgewater Homes is a Cork-headquartered developer with public activity across development, planning, design, construction, sales/marketing, customer service, and post-build property management.

Its currently marketed schemes are:

- Waterfall Avenue, Bishopstown
- Waterfall Heights, Bishopstown
- The Orchard, Rochestown/Douglas
- Glenwood, Strawhall, Fermoy
- Ocean Drive, Ballycotton

The company therefore appears to have the multi-scheme, homeowner-facing complexity that OpenHouse is designed for.

## 20-second opening

> OpenHouse helps developers give each homeowner a clear, branded record of the home from handover onwards. Instead of critical information being spread across packs, emails, site teams and aftercare threads, it brings the completion evidence, homeowner guidance, issues and ongoing home context into one premium, resident-facing place. Carol thought there might be a useful fit, but I wanted to start by understanding how Bridgewater currently handles that experience across the live schemes.

## Questions to earn the right to demo

1. How is handover information currently assembled and shared for a new homeowner?
2. After completion, where do homeowner questions, snags, warranty requests, and documentation actually live?
3. Is the process consistent across Waterfall, The Orchard, Glenwood, and Ocean Drive, or does each scheme run differently?
4. Which part creates the most avoidable work or customer friction for the sales, site, or customer-care team?
5. If the next 12 months of homeowner experience went exceptionally well, what would be visibly different for Bridgewater and the buyer?

## Relevant OpenHouse surfaces

Show only the surfaces their answers make relevant:

- branded homeowner handover and home record;
- documentation, warranty, BER, and O&M evidence;
- photo-led handover and issue context;
- homeowner question/support experience;
- snag and aftercare workflow;
- portfolio consistency and a reusable scheme template.

## Initial pilot shape

Do not lead with a portfolio-wide contract. If there is genuine pain, propose a narrowly measurable pilot on one upcoming or active scheme:

- clear implementation owner;
- one defined homeowner cohort;
- agreed handover/aftercare workflow;
- evidence and support measures agreed before launch;
- review after a short operational cycle.

## Follow-up standard

Send a tailored one-page recap within 24 hours of the meeting. It should name their stated workflow, the exact friction point, the relevant OpenHouse surface, and the smallest credible next step.
