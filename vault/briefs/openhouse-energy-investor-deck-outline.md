# OpenHouse energy intelligence investor deck outline

## Deck thesis
OpenHouse is becoming the intelligence layer between people, homes, and energy systems. It starts with developer-provisioned new builds, where the product has full build context from day one, then expands into retrofit homes, smart-meter integrations, batteries, estates, and national-scale energy intelligence.

## Tone
- Premium, grounded, inevitable.
- Less "AI chatbot", more "operating layer for modern homes".
- Use simple visual metaphors: home model, energy flows, stakeholder loop, compounding network.
- Keep every slide anchored in business value: bills, comfort, risk, aftercare, data moat.
