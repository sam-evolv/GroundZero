# Aire six-month autonomous-company mission

## Mission

Build Aire into a useful consumer personal agent that removes the junk of life and returns time, while using the same agentic operating system to make Aire itself the most credible example of an AI-operated company in Ireland.

The product promise is simple: automate the administrative work people hate so they have more room to think, create and live. Examples include unnecessary email, spreadsheets, presentations, insurance calls, receipt organisation and other awkward personal administration.

The company proof is stronger: Aire should become excellent at automation by progressively automating its own software-company operations. If the company can run safely, transparently and reliably with AI carrying most repeatable work under Sam's ownership and oversight, that becomes evidence that the product is real.

## Founder ambition

Sam's six-month ambition is to become recognised as Ireland's leading practical voice on AI and automation. The route is not commentary alone. It is to do something visibly difficult that others have not executed, then show the evidence.

The proposed public story is an Irish founder building an AI-operated software company, proving the operating model through a controlled public experiment, and using that proof to establish Aire, Sam and the underlying operating system as credible automation leaders.

The desired downstream effects are:

- trust in Aire because the company uses its own automation system;
- national media, podcast, radio and founder interest;
- inbound from business owners asking how parts of their companies could be automated;
- inbound from software founders who want to build with less operational headcount;
- a defensible reputation based on execution, not generic AI content;
- optional consulting, implementation, partnerships and investment opportunities.

## Strategic logic

Ireland has a smaller startup and technical talent pool than larger ecosystems, and Irish public culture can be sceptical of ambitious claims. That can be an advantage if the proof is unusually concrete.

Few people in Ireland currently combine Sam's depth of practical agent use, founder context and willingness to build publicly. The opportunity is to become the person associated with useful, evidence-backed automation before the category becomes crowded.

The company and product reinforce each other:

1. Aire removes unwanted administration from customers' lives.
2. Aire's internal operating system removes repeatable administration from running Aire.
3. The company records what AI did, what required approval, what failed and what was repaired.
4. Repeated internal proof improves the product and produces credible public evidence.
5. Public proof creates distribution, trust and inbound automation opportunities.

## Six-month sequence

### Phase 1: Build a product people want

- Complete the truthful Chat, Work and Profile product around Hermes.
- Win the first real customers and observe the jobs they repeatedly delegate.
- Use Aire daily as Sam's own assistant and company operating layer.
- Automate internal workflows as they become stable, not before they are understood.
- Preserve founder control over money, legal commitments, customer promises, security and publication.

### Phase 2: Build the AI-operated company

- Map every recurring company workflow, owner, trigger, evidence, failure mode and approval boundary.
- Move workflows from manual to supervised, then bounded asynchronous, then event-driven operation only after repeated success.
- Cover product operations, testing, support preparation, finance administration, content preparation, analytics, incident triage, documentation and internal planning.
- Keep customer-facing and consequential actions approval-gated until evidence earns narrower autonomy.
- Build one auditable company-control view showing work attempted, work completed, approvals, failures, intervention and recovery.

### Phase 3: Rehearse the public proof

- Run one controlled two-to-three-week rehearsal while Sam remains available.
- Correct failures and repeat the rehearsal.
- Accumulate at least one month of reliable operating evidence before making the headline claim.
- Define an emergency stop, incident response, customer protection and public correction protocol.
- Commission an independent review of the evidence and exact operating boundaries.

### Phase 4: Public demonstration

The proposed campaign is a founder absence experiment. Sam leaves normal day-to-day operation for roughly two weeks while Aire's operating system runs the company under predefined safety and intervention rules.

A public surface may show, in sanitised form:

- work received and completed;
- content prepared and, where pre-authorised, published;
- product changes proposed, tested and reviewed;
- support and operational queues;
- system prompts and operating procedures being improved;
- failures, retries, escalations and founder interventions;
- exact areas that remain human-controlled.

The media proposition is not a theatrical livestream of fake activity. It is a falsifiable operating experiment with an audit trail.

## Claim standard

The strongest defensible headline may ultimately be "Ireland's first AI-operated company" rather than "fully autonomous company" if Sam, contractors, providers or approval gates remain operationally necessary.

No public claim should outrun the evidence. Before using "fully autonomous" or equivalent language, the experiment must define:

- what counts as the company running;
- which functions are in scope;
- which decisions remain reserved to Sam;
- every human intervention during the proof window;
- service providers and contractors involved;
- safety, legal, financial and security boundaries;
- uptime, completion, error and recovery metrics;
- how an independent person could audit the result.

A narrower claim with overwhelming proof will create more trust and status than an absolute claim that can be punctured in one interview.

## Media and distribution thesis

Once the evidence exists, Sam can approach Irish podcasts, radio, business media and technology reporters with a concrete experiment rather than a product pitch.

The offer can include a bounded, free automation diagnostic for a participating media organisation after coverage, without promising implementation or compromising editorial independence.

Every public asset should point back to the product promise: Aire gives people time back by removing the administrative junk of life.

## Technology thesis

The six-month horizon matters because model capability, open-weight models, local inference and Hermes Agent are improving quickly. The plan should benefit from those changes without depending on a specific unreleased model or hardware purchase.

The durable work starts now:

- domain knowledge;
- operating procedures;
- workflow traces;
- evals;
- permissions;
- approvals;
- audit logs;
- failure recovery;
- customer proof;
- distribution.

Future frontier-level local models may reduce cost and increase 24-hour operating capacity. They do not remove the need for the operating system and evidence layer.

## What this mission is not

- It is not a reason to delay customer acquisition for six months.
- It is not permission to manufacture visible activity for a livestream.
- It is not a claim that an AI system owns responsibility, liability or judgment.
- It is not a consultancy pivot before Aire has product proof.
- It is not broad autonomy before supervised workflows have earned it.
- It is not dependent on becoming technically famous before the product creates value.

## Immediate operating consequence

Every meaningful Aire workflow should now be evaluated twice:

1. Does this remove hated work and return time to the customer?
2. Can the same pattern safely increase Aire's own operating autonomy and become auditable proof?
