- The assistant's earlier framing was too conservative and contradicted Sam on market structure, personal finances and normal meeting conduct without bringing new evidence. That was an error.

## Current context

Sam is mentally exhausted by trying to make OpenHouse commercially acceptable without producing independent revenue. The core frustration is not product capability. It is the lack of one external developer willing to pay or formally commit.

Verified current position:

- OpenHouse has €0 external revenue.
- Bridgewater, Hallmark and Cairn have said no.
- Repeated attempts to reach a decision-maker at Glenveagh have not produced a meeting.
- O'Flynn Construction is not a credible buyer route because of direct competitive dynamics and Sam's first-hand market judgement.
- Sam estimates that these closed or inaccessible routes represent approximately 15 per cent of annual Irish new-home output.
- OpenHouse has conservative internal operating proof: 259 property-specific questions across 79 home contexts in four Longview developments.
- YC and the NDRC pre-accelerator remain pending as last verified.
- Sam recalls being told by a High Potential Start-Up contact that revenue or investment would materially strengthen the funding case. This is founder-supplied context, not a formal funding offer.

## Durable decision

The next phase is founder-led revenue creation, not another broad product-build cycle.

The target is one external developer commitment. The order is:

1. Continue pursuing the remaining credible Irish routes where access exists.
2. Build the UK and US pipeline in parallel rather than waiting for the concentrated Irish market to open.
3. Use Sam's direct network aggressively and make specific asks for meetings and introductions.
4. Run outbound at sufficient volume to create conversations while keeping the buyer and offer specific.
5. Freeze speculative features. Product changes must be tied to a named buyer, pilot requirement, release-safety gate or measured usage problem.

## Commercial interpretation

Building is not the current bottleneck. Founder sales is.

One paid external deployment would change investor, accelerator and hiring conversations more than another deck or product surface. Sam has explicitly decided to accept the discomfort and volume required to force that commercial proof.

## Thirty-day commercial proof loop

### Target outcome

- two developer discovery conversations;
- one written one-scheme proposal;
- one paid pilot, signed commitment or explicit rejection that materially changes the offer.

### Buyer and offer

- Buyer: residential developer handover, customer-care or aftercare owner.
- Initial offer: one measured scheme-launch and aftercare workflow.
- Commercial model: implementation fee plus a per-home fee, with roughly €99 per home still treated as an unvalidated hypothesis.
- First ask: a 30-minute workflow conversation, not a deck review or full platform pitch.

### Sequence

1. Remaining credible Irish routes where Sam has access or a plausible decision-maker.
2. Direct-network asks for introductions, including Gavin and Sean Gallagher where appropriate.
3. UK developers with a visible handover or aftercare owner and a scheme that can support a design-partner pilot.
4. US homebuilders and relevant channels using the same specific handover and aftercare proposition.
5. Keep the offer narrow enough to learn from responses, but do not artificially cap outreach volume.

### Weekly evidence

Track only:

- targeted messages sent;
- replies;
- conversations booked;
- qualified workflow problems;
- proposals issued;
- paid or signed commitments;
- reasons for rejection.

Do not count impressions, general encouragement, deck refinements or new features as commercial progress.

## Meeting conduct

Sam's private urgency is not evidence that he would behave desperately in a buyer meeting. He has extensive direct developer experience and can separate private venting from professional sales conduct. Do not lecture him on basic meeting behaviour unless there is a specific observed risk or he asks for rehearsal.

Revenue materially strengthens the funding case. Use any paid pilot or signed commitment as fresh evidence for NDRC and the relevant High Potential Start-Up route. The current January decision assumes €100,000 in funding plus the expected €10,000 bonus.

Treat that as founder-certified financial context. Do not reopen or second-guess it unless Sam supplies changed figures or asks for a runway analysis.

## Founder role insight

Sam's strongest founder skills are product vision, systems thinking, innovation and one-to-one persuasion. The weakness is not an inability to sell. It is avoiding the repeated, uncomfortable prospecting work required to create enough one-to-one sales opportunities.

A future commercial cofounder or senior hire may be valuable, but only after Sam has personally learned the sale, buyer language, objections and implementation boundary. Hiring someone to discover the market from scratch would outsource the learning OpenHouse most needs.
