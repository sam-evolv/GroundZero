# Sam 90-day cash-to-authority plan

## Diagnosis
Sam does not need another venture or generic AI consultancy. The constraint is a split portfolio: full-time employment preserves runway while consuming attention; several ventures create option value but do not yet create sufficient personal cash; new ideas and research can mimic momentum while fragmenting compounding.

## Decision frame
For 90 days, operate only three lanes:

1. **Runway:** retain the job until a pre-agreed exit gate is achieved.
2. **Cash:** one productised, repeatable managed service with short sales cycles.
3. **Equity:** OpenHouse remains the long-term company, with a single evidence goal.

No fourth company or new active project may enter without replacing an existing lane.

## Cash engine: channel-led AI revenue recovery desk
Do not sell generic AI consulting directly to sceptical SMEs. Sell a tightly scoped, software-enabled service through trusted distribution partners such as regional accountancy firms, CRM/web agencies, MSPs, and sector advisers.

### Initial offer
**AI Revenue Recovery Desk for high-ticket service firms**

> In 10 days, we connect enquiry, missed-call, quote and follow-up workflows so valuable leads are answered, chased and reported without falling through the cracks.

Target initial vertical: renewable / building-services installers, where Sam has real domain credibility through [[companies/evolv-renewables]] and the work creates reusable learning for [[companies/openhouse-ai]]. It is separate from trying to sell OpenHouse itself.

### Commercial shape
- €3,500 fixed implementation
- €1,500/month managed optimisation and reporting
- 6-month minimum term
- narrow delivery promise; no vague "AI transformation"
- risk reversal: if the core workflow is not live in 10 working days, waive the first monthly fee

### 90-day target
- Month 1: two channel partners and one paid implementation
- Month 2: three total clients; €10,500 setup cash collected and €4,500 MRR contracted
- Month 3: four total clients; €14,000 setup cash collected and €6,000 MRR contracted

The target is not simply €40k annualised salary replacement. The practical exit gate is €6k MRR under signed six-month terms, plus cash collected and a credible pipeline. This absorbs self-employment risk, churn and operating costs.

## Distribution system
The sales motion is partner-led, not cold outbound to individual owner-operators.

1. Select 20 likely distribution partners.
2. Offer each a white-labelled revenue-recovery system for their existing clients.
3. Give the partner 20% recurring revenue or a flat referral fee.
4. Run a 30-minute prospect diagnostic; collect a paid €500 deposit credited against implementation.
5. Publish anonymous before/after operating metrics and case studies.

This leverages a trusted relationship rather than asking a sceptical buyer to take a chance on a new AI consultant.

## Authority engine
Sam's AI brand must be downstream of real operator work, not a separate content job.

- Publish one sharp weekly field note: a real workflow, result, failure, or lesson from delivery.
- Publish one monthly Irish AI Operator Briefing for owners/operators.
- Avoid generic AI hot takes and policy commentary while employed.
- Build the brand around a defensible identity: **the Irish AI operator who turns messy commercial workflows into revenue and capacity.**

The golf-management opportunity is relationship and credibility capital, not an excuse to launch a separate golf product or force sales conversations during tournament week.

## What not to do now
- quit the job based on hope, a single pilot, or a bad week
- wait for investment to make a cash problem disappear
- launch another broad consultancy, media brand, agency, or SaaS
- sell to enterprise committees as the near-term cash plan
- use public AI content as procrastination from commercial proof
