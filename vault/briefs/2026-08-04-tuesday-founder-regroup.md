## Candid read

There is no need to "start OpenHouse again." The product, evidence pack, secure YC reviewer experience and four-scheme operating proof already exist. The company needs commercial repetition around them.

The risk this week is fragmentation: developer outreach, Care, DTC, funding and product work can each feel productive while none produces a buyer. One commercial loop must own the week.

The right loop is:

**qualified developer → discovery conversation → one-scheme proposal → paid pilot, written commitment or decision-changing rejection**

## Ranked work for this week

### 1. Bridgewater now

- Sam sends the ready Carol reply.
- On introduction, ask for the person responsible for residential delivery, handover, customer care or sales operations.
- Lead with their current process and costs, not a software tour.
- Use the verified Longview proof pack only when useful in the conversation.

### 2. Build the next qualified developer queue

Research a focused list of approximately 20 Irish and UK residential developers that:

- deliver enough homes for handover and aftercare repetition to matter;
- visibly operate customer-care, warranty, resident or aftercare workflows;
- have a reachable named decision-maker;
- are not already covered by a stronger warm route;
- can plausibly trial one scheme without enterprise-wide procurement.

For each, identify the development evidence, likely operational pain, relevant person, warm-path possibility and one specific reason OpenHouse fits. A pile of names without this evidence is not useful.

### 3. DTC proof, not subscription build

Define one homeowner result that is useful enough to trigger payment. Run the existing founder-home activation contract before writing a recurring product. A €5 monthly price would require ongoing recurring value and retention proof that does not yet exist.

### 4. Care as a contained interview lane

Interview or research one credible solar, heat-pump or home-systems installer. Test whether fragmented handover, homeowner education, service history or support calls cost them enough to buy. Do not build more Care surface until one buyer problem is confirmed.
