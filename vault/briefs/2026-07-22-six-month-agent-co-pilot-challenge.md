# Six-Month Agent Co-Pilot Challenge

## Current context

Sam is deliberately choosing a six-month experiment in using Hermes and Ground Zero as a structured external brain. The purpose is not to delegate life, seek generic reassurance, or create an AI-content narrative. The purpose is to reduce ambient cognitive overload, protect attention, strengthen follow-through, and generate evidence of whether an AI-supported operating system improves health, work, cash, founder execution, relationships, and personal capacity.

The immediate felt problem is not lack of ideas. It is too much unprocessed context, emotional urgency, pressure to generate income, and a tendency for momentum to rise and fall with perceived progress. The system must turn raw context into a small number of grounded actions, not another source of complexity.

## Decision

Run a private-by-default six-month agent co-pilot experiment from 22 July 2026 to 22 January 2027.

The experiment begins with a 14-day proof phase. Public writing, a Substack launch, LinkedIn positioning, or any claim that AI has transformed Sam's life is deferred until the system has produced evidence rather than a story.

## Hypothesis

If Sam uses a short daily voice/capture ritual, a weekly decision review, and Ground Zero as the source of truth, then he will:

- execute more consistently on health and work;
- reduce re-litigation and avoidable decision fatigue;
- make clearer cash and portfolio decisions;
- maintain an evidence trail that can later support a truthful founder/AI-operator case study;
- retain agency, relationships, privacy, and final judgement.

## Anti-hypothesis and failure signs

The experiment is failing if it creates more talking than doing, increases anxiety, makes Sam defer values or responsibility to AI, creates a large dashboard without changed behaviour, or distracts from job performance, relationships, body recovery, cash, and the one active commercial proof loop.

If that happens, reduce capture frequency, reduce tracked metrics, remove automations, and return to a one-page weekly plan.

## Operating agreement

This extends, rather than replaces, [[decisions/ai-founder-coach-operating-agreement-2026-07-14]] and [[context/founder-execution-os]].

### Hermes does

- capture raw voice context and distinguish fact, claim, assumption, inference, decision, and experiment;
- preserve relevant context in Ground Zero with source and date;
- summarise the one decision-changing thread, not every thought;
- challenge distraction, avoidance, story-over-evidence, and excessive WIP;
- prepare research, plans, tracking, and drafts;
- maintain private evidence artefacts and review trends;
- ask one material question only when it changes the next action.

### Sam does

- chooses values, goals, priorities, risk tolerance, and final decisions;
- approves every external, financial, legal, reputational, publishing, relationship, and production action;
- keeps the system honest by reporting missed commitments and changing conditions;
- uses human professional support where it is needed.

### Never delegate to Hermes

- diagnosis, therapy, crisis care, or replacing human relationships;
- investing, trading, moving money, accepting financial products, or tax/legal advice;
- passwords, account secrets, employer-confidential material, or private third-party data;
- outreach, publishing, investor/partner contact, or representations made in Sam's name without explicit approval;
- final career, relationship, or health decisions.

## Active-goal rule

The scorecard has several domains, but a full-time founder does **not** optimise all of them at once. Each six-week cycle has a maximum of two active outcome goals:

1. One health/body-composition goal.
2. One work, business, or financial goal.

For the first cycle, the active outcomes are:

1. **Health adherence and recomposition.** Execute the existing training/nutrition block, then adjust only from trend evidence.
2. **Cash authority.** Establish the actual financial picture and run the already-defined, bounded online-first bridge/application experiment.

Day-job quality, relationships, and OpenHouse are guardrails and evidence sources in this cycle. They require maintenance and clear commitments, but are not separate optimisation projects. OpenHouse receives a tightly bounded preparation action only when it displaces neither job integrity nor the active cash outcome.

## Six-month scorecard

The core result is not a single happiness score. It is a small evidence set across five areas.

### 1. Body and energy

- seven-day average bodyweight and waist trend;
- strength-session completion and progression;
- step average, sleep average, and injury signals;
- consistent fortnightly physique photos.

### 2. Day-job integrity

- weekly commitments completed;
- one explicit contribution or deliverable that matters to the employer each week;
- work stress and disengagement noted as context, without using the experiment as an excuse to neglect the role.

### 3. Personal cash and financial clarity

- actual cash position, essential burn, liabilities, and investment inventory captured once;
- realised side income, task allocation, applications, or interviews, not imagined opportunity;
- no investment actions based solely on AI recommendation.

### 4. Founder and commercial proof

- one active cash/proof motion at a time, with an external evidence threshold;
- OpenHouse remains the long-term equity company, but must have a defined evidence goal before more work;
- Cara stays founder-dogfooding first; OpenBook and Evolv only receive bounded work that improves a chosen metric;
- customer, buyer, distribution, product, or interview evidence counts. Research, dashboards, polishing, and agent infrastructure do not.

### 5. Capacity and relationships

- a short daily energy/groundedness note only when useful;
- protected time, sleep, training, and relationship commitments are treated as capacity infrastructure, not optional rewards;
- no monitoring or scoring of other people.

## Evidence basis

This is a pragmatic personal operating experiment, not a clinical trial. The design uses a small set of evidence-backed mechanisms:

- **Goal monitoring:** monitoring progress tends to improve goal attainment, especially when progress is recorded or reported. This supports a minimal scorecard, not obsessive measurement. Harkin et al., 2016: https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000025
- **Implementation intentions:** specifying when, where, and how a next action happens improves follow-through. This supports a concrete definition of done and a time box on daily cards. Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1
- **Habit formation:** repetition in a stable context matters more than intensity or a perfect streak. This supports a boring, repeatable daily rhythm. Lally et al., 2010: https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674
- **Cognitive offloading:** external memory can be useful, but can change what people remember and retrieve unaided. This is why the agent cannot be the only memory or authority. Risko & Gilbert, 2016: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2016.07.002
- **Human oversight:** the NIST AI RMF supports documented governance, measurement, and human control rather than blind automation. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **Privacy minimisation:** personal data should be limited to what is necessary. Health, financial, employer, and third-party detail therefore remain local, excluded from Git, and redacted before any future public proof. European Commission: https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/legal-grounds-processing-data/sensitive-data/what-personal-data-considered-sensitive_en

## Public-proof rule

Do not start a public diary now. A first public piece can be considered after the 14-day review only if there is an honest artefact, a specific lesson, and no employer, customer, financial, relationship, or privacy risk.

The later case study should show the system, the evidence, the misses, the boundaries, and the outcomes. It should not claim that an AI “ran” Sam's life. The credible thesis is: **a human-led operating system, using agents for capture, challenge, research, and follow-through, improved the quality and speed of decisions.**

## First 14 days: proof phase

### Daily, maximum 10 minutes of deliberate admin

1. Send raw voice context when useful. No requirement to narrate every thought.
2. Hermes returns only:
   - the one decision or tension that matters;
   - today’s one revenue/proof move;
   - today’s one delivery/job move;
   - body/recovery requirement if relevant;
   - one question only if needed.
3. Log morning weight, training/steps, major food deviations, sleep, and a short end-of-day outcome note.

### Weekly, 45 minutes

- facts first: body, cash, work, business evidence, capacity;
- close or extend due commitments;
- name what was avoided;
- choose three observable commitments for the next week;
- explicitly decide what does not get attention.

### Proof-phase commitments

- Health: execute the active four-week recomposition plan and capture enough data to adjust it.
- Job: restore a weekly work plan and deliver one meaningful employer outcome each week.
- Cash: execute the existing online-first bridge/application experiment in [[briefs/2026-07-21-escape-velocity-income-engine]] with a fixed time cap. No new cash scheme.
- Equity: define one OpenHouse evidence action. Do not reopen all companies at once.
- System: make the capture-and-review loop lighter if it is not changing action.
