# Vibiz Connector source audit and Aire lessons

## Why this matters

Vibiz is a useful live example of a vertical software product turning a general AI client into an operator. The interface is Claude, but the execution layer is a remote MCP server with a large, structured tool surface and persistent business context.

The durable lesson for Aire is not to copy a broad "one prompt runs the company" claim. It is to make complex work feel like one delegation while preserving real progress, approval, evidence and receipts underneath.

## What is substantively real

The public documentation exposes 59 MCP tools across six surfaces:

- Generation: images, carousels, product shots, UGC clips, video ads, funnels, ICPs and offers
- Workspace: business context and branded email
- Analytics: social performance and posting-time analysis
- Inbox: read and reply to DMs and comments, bulk replies and standing comment automations
- Social: trend research, account and post listing, publishing and scheduling
- Meta ads: campaign trees, status control, duplication, deletion, creative management, boosting and paid campaign launch

The system is more than a wrapper around image generation. It combines:

- a workspace and brand model
- large typed tool coverage
- OAuth and API-key access
- creation, deployment, publishing and paid-media operations
- read surfaces for analytics and inboxes
- repeatable automation within the vertical

The product site positions this as a three-step loop:

1. Drop a URL or idea.
2. Build the stack.
3. Launch and get paid.

Its plans are $24, $40 and $64 per member per month, with monthly credits and a stated 2% service fee on ad spend. This supports a broader Aire economics lesson: expensive outcome work may need visible allowances or work packs rather than being hidden inside a low consumer subscription.

## Strong product patterns to adopt for Aire

### 1. Sell the role shift, not the tools

Vibiz's strongest line is not "59 MCP tools". It is "Claude was your copilot. Claude is now your operator."

Aire should use its own truthful version:

- from advice to completed work
- Aire does the work, you keep the decision
- ask once, see the work move, approve what matters

The external promise should remain ordinary language. The tool catalogue stays underneath.

### 2. One delegation can conceal a structured plan

The user gives one outcome request. The product decomposes it into market research, brand, audience, offer, creative, site, distribution and analytics.

For Aire, one user request should create one authoritative Work object with:

- intended outcome
- decomposed steps
- live human-language milestones
- blocked or approval-required steps
- delivered artifacts
- external-action receipts
- elapsed work time and conservative Time Given Back

This directly reinforces the accepted Chat, Work and Profile architecture.

### 3. Show movement through recognizable outcomes

The Vibiz montage works because every beat is legible: brand kit, customer profile, creative, site, campaign, payment. It does not show raw logs.

Aire should similarly show milestone verbs and useful previews, not runtime jargon:

- found the policy
- compared the renewal options
- prepared the cancellation email
- waiting for your approval to send
- sent and saved the receipt

### 4. Meet users in the interface they already use

Vibiz lets Claude, Claude Code and Cursor become the front end. The connector expands an existing relationship instead of asking the user to learn a new automation builder.

Aire should be the primary consumer relationship, but use the same principle internally:

- bring capabilities into the conversation just in time
- let vertical services appear as skills, not separate products
- avoid an integration catalogue as the main experience
- preserve one identity and one task state across devices

### 5. Narrow vertical context creates execution quality

Vibiz can act because its workspace contains brand, offers, products, audiences, connected accounts and campaign history. The model is not starting from a blank chat every time.

Aire's equivalent is inspectable personal context plus live Work state. The advantage is trusted continuity and better outcomes, not the amount of data stored.

### 6. Connect creation to operation and feedback

Vibiz spans create, publish, manage, read performance and respond. That is a closed loop rather than a one-shot generator.

Aire should prioritise complete loops such as:

- receive renewal notice
- find current cover and alternatives
- prepare recommendation
- obtain approval
- send or call
- store confirmation
- monitor completion

The receipt and follow-up matter as much as the draft.

## What Aire should deliberately do better

### 1. Prove the chain instead of implying it

Vibiz jumps from prompt to montage to payment notifications. Aire's proof should preserve:

- original request
- actual actions taken
- time and cost
- sources and artifacts
- approvals
- external receipts
- human corrections
- failures and recovery

This is essential to the six-month AI-operated company mission. A narrower claim with overwhelming evidence is stronger than cinematic autonomy.

### 2. Make consequential boundaries explicit

The Vibiz tools include publishing, direct messages, standing reply automations, deleting campaigns and launching paid ads. The public tool descriptions contain useful fail-closed guidance for wrong brands, target IDs, country selection and default budgets, but the reviewed documentation does not state a universal per-action confirmation requirement before sending, publishing, deleting or spending. The host client may add its own gate, but that is not clear from the tool reference.

Aire can make this a product advantage:

- preview before send or publish
- exact audience, account and budget shown
- explicit per-action approval for consequential work
- named recurring scopes with expiry and revocation
- receipt after execution
- pause, cancel and recovery controls

### 3. Separate generated assets from real market proof

Generated UGC, landing pages and dashboards are output. They are not evidence that customers bought, that ads performed or that a business exists.

Aire's public proof should distinguish:

- generated
- reviewed
- published
- delivered
- accepted
- paid
- repeated successfully

### 4. Avoid the generic autonomous-company trap

Vibiz is strongest as a vertical marketing and launch operator. The "entire business" claim is much broader than the demonstrated scope.

Aire should not compete by claiming broader autonomy sooner. It should win through cross-domain delegation, trust, continuity, approvals and truthful completion evidence.

## Immediate implications for Aire

1. Keep Chat as the delegation surface and Work as the visible, authoritative execution record.
2. Design one signature demo around a hated real-life job, not a tour of Hermes capability.
3. Show six to eight recognizable milestones and one real external receipt in under 45 seconds.
4. Use cinematic compression only after recording the complete auditable run.
5. Add outcome-state vocabulary to the presentation contract: prepared, waiting for approval, executed, receipt saved, failed, recovered.
6. Keep connectors just in time and invisible until the job needs them.
7. Treat paid media, purchases, sending, booking, deletion and account changes as explicit approval events.
8. Instrument cost per completed outcome and support premium work allowances instead of assuming every task fits the base subscription.
9. For Aire's own company-operation proof, make each recurring workflow callable through the same bounded tool and Work-object contract used by customers.
10. Tell the stronger story: Vibiz demonstrates that vertical software can turn a general model into an operator. Aire's opportunity is to turn the whole consumer agent into a trusted operator across life and work.
