## The application voice

The answers should sound like Sam at his clearest, not like a copywriter and not like a transcript.

Use:

- "I saw..."
- "I built..."
- "It is live..."
- "The next thing I need to prove is..."
- Plain language, short sentences and exact numbers.

Avoid:

- "AI platform", "operating system", "ecosystem", "digital twin" and "revolutionising" before the concrete product is understood.
- Symmetrical consultant prose.
- Long competitor lists without a structural insight.
- Repeating the same metrics in several answers.
- Calling records, linked accounts or agreements users, customers or completed handovers.
- Sounding American or trying to imitate YC founders.

## The answer cadence

For most free-text questions use three beats:

1. Direct answer in the first sentence.
2. One or two specific facts proving it.
3. The honest boundary or next test, only when relevant.

Typical length is two to five sentences. The answer should stop once every clause in the prompt is covered. Do not add a concluding slogan.

Example structure:

> I built OpenHouse after repeatedly seeing property context disappear at handover. I am one of three people responsible for running Longview Estates, where we have taken a planned 750-plus-home development from greenfield to more than 200 completed homes. The internal deployment proves I can build and operate the product. The next proof is whether an independent developer will pay for it.

## Where the personal YC motivation belongs

Sam's determination matters, but it should not dominate the application. YC is selecting the founder and company, not rewarding admiration for YC.

Do not include:

- alumni worship
- a pilgrimage metaphor
- YC as proof of personal worth
- how badly Sam wants the status

One short authentic line can appear in "What convinced you to apply" if it strengthens the current company transition:

> I had treated YC as something for other founders until I realised that was a limit I had imposed on myself, not a reason not to apply.

Then return immediately to evidence:

> OpenHouse is live, but I now need to turn internal operating proof into repeatable external sales. YC is the strongest place for me to make that transition quickly and build a serious US route.
