## Likely short-answer framing if later pages ask for narrative

### One-line description

OpenHouse is the construction-stage intelligence layer for modern homes, starting as a practical property assistant and expanding into home energy intelligence.

### Problem

Modern homes are becoming more complex energy systems, but homeowners are not given an operating layer. Developers hold the best context about each home during construction, but that data is lost after handover.

### Solution

OpenHouse captures source-of-truth home context at construction, turns it into a living home model, and gives homeowners a practical assistant for questions, issues, warranties and energy guidance.

### Market

Ireland is the proof market through developer-provisioned new builds. The UK is the scale market through larger developers, housing associations, smart-meter homes and higher energy-pressure households.

### Business model

Developers pay a low-friction provisioning fee, starting at €99/home. Homeowners create recurring upside through a €9.99/month subscription, with a future agentic optimisation tier once connected-system trust matures.

### Why now

Homes are becoming energy systems because of heat pumps, EVs, solar, batteries, smart meters and changing tariffs. The operating layer needs home-specific context, not generic dashboards.

### Why NDRC

NDRC provides the investment, mentor pressure, investor readiness, founder network and Irish ecosystem credibility needed to turn a strong product thesis into a fundable, repeatable proof case.

### 12-week NDRC goal

Prove that construction-stage home intelligence can become a repeatable wedge: create the living home model v1, onboard a developer scheme or credible pilot path, validate homeowner assistant usage, test issue intake, and prepare an investor-ready case for Ireland-to-UK scale.
