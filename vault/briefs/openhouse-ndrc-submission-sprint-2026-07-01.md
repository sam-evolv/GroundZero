## NDRC terms and criteria extracted

### What NDRC says it is for

- Ambitious founders building globally scalable technology companies.
- Early-stage Irish and international startups with global ambition.
- Teams as early as formation, or ready to move fast.
- Mentorship-driven programme with hands-on support, investor connectivity, and access to Ireland's founder community and alumni network.

### Funding

- €100k founder-friendly uncapped SAFE investment.
- Split into two €50k installments.
- First €50k at programme start.
- Second €50k upon completion of the accelerator goal set at programme outset.
- NDRC states the full intention is to invest €100k in each accelerator startup.

### Delivery

- Hybrid programme.
- Startups are asked to be on-site at Dogpatch Labs in Dublin for 3 core weeks.
- The rest is virtual.

### Application and review process

- Application is competitive.
- Assessed through online application and follow-up video interview for shortlisted applicants.
- Shortlisted applicants are contacted to arrange interview within 3 weeks of applications closing.
- Final selection notifications are sent within 4 weeks from application closing date.
- No personalised feedback if rejected, but applicants may be redirected to Pre-Accelerator if considered too early.

### Location eligibility

- Irish startups fit.
- International startups may apply if they intend to relocate/incorporate in Ireland or are headquartered and controlled in Ireland.
- OpenHouse fits this location requirement because Sam and OpenHouse are Ireland-based.

### Main risk

The deck must not look like a generic handover portal, generic chatbot, or vague AI-energy thesis. It must show a fundable wedge:

> construction-stage context → everyday property assistant → living home model → energy intelligence → trusted optimisation → Ireland proof → UK scale

### Do not apply if

- The deck PDF still has browser export artifacts, clipped headings, URLs, page numbers, portrait pages, or inconsistent branding.
- The deck invents traction, pilots, revenue, investor names, savings, logos, or commitments.
- The application story cannot clearly answer why OpenHouse is globally scalable.
- The deck hides the day-one utility and jumps straight to speculative agentic energy control.

## NDRC-specific deck objective

This is not a generic investor deck. It must convince NDRC that OpenHouse is:

1. ambitious enough for accelerator investment,
2. grounded enough to be credible now,
3. Irish enough to be ecosystem-relevant,
4. globally scalable enough to justify accelerator backing,
5. founder-market-fit enough that Sam can execute,
6. at the right stage for mentorship, network and a €100k SAFE.
