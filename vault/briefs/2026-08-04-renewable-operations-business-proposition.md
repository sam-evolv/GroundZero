# Renewable Operations Intelligence

## The proposition

Build an Ireland-first managed operations company for renewable asset owners.

The company would sit above existing O&M contractors and technology vendors, connecting plant data, field evidence and maintenance activity so owners can identify losses, direct action and verify that problems were solved.

The first market is independent owners and investment vehicles with approximately 20–250 MW of operational solar across multiple sites. They are large enough to lose meaningful money through weak operations, but often too small to justify a complete internal asset-management team or enterprise software deployment.

The company does not begin as a solar cleaner, drone operator or generic software platform. It sells one outcome:

> Fewer unexplained losses and a verified operating record for every asset, fault, intervention and recovery.

## Why now

- Ireland had approximately 1.59 GW of utility-scale solar connected by May 2026, within 2.7 GW of total connected solar.
- Ireland targets 8 GW by 2030, creating a rapidly expanding 30-year operations and maintenance base.
- The adjacent UK market had 22.8 GW of solar by June 2026, with roughly 59% estimated to be ground-mounted.
- Owners increasingly manage mixed equipment, portals, contractors, warranties and reporting requirements.
- Thermal drones, daily satellite imagery, inexpensive sensors, robotic field equipment and capable AI models now make a more integrated service economically possible.
- Existing O&M and monitoring products validate the spend, but many owners still lack a reliable link between an alarm, the responsible contractor, evidence of repair and verified performance recovery.

## The customer problem

Renewable owners often have SCADA, inverter portals, meters, weather data, contractor tickets, warranty documents, inspection PDFs and spreadsheets without one trusted operating picture.

The resulting failures include:

- faults and underperformance identified late;
- repeated alarms without permanent resolution;
- unclear responsibility across owner, EPC, OEM, O&M provider and grid;
- slow warranty claims caused by missing serial numbers, readings or photographs;
- disagreement over availability, curtailment and lost generation;
- cleaning or vegetation work commissioned without a measured economic case;
- monthly investor and lender reporting assembled manually;
- no reliable proof that a completed intervention restored output.

## The productised managed service

### 1. Portfolio operations desk

- Read-only ingestion from SCADA, inverter portals, meters, weather and market sources.
- A verified asset hierarchy and component register.
- Generation, export, availability and curtailment reconciliation.
- Business-hours anomaly review and prioritisation by safety, affected capacity and estimated lost MWh.
- Owner-visible work orders assigned to the responsible contractor or OEM.
- Monthly operating, investor and lender reporting.

### 2. Field assurance network

Specialist partners perform work while the company controls the operating record and verifies the result:

- thermal and RGB drone inspections;
- electrical testing and corrective maintenance;
- vegetation, drainage, fencing and access work;
- evidence-based module cleaning;
- security and environmental inspections;
- warranty evidence and post-repair verification.

This keeps initial capital and certification requirements low while giving the asset owner one accountable operating interface.

### 3. Evidence and intelligence layer

Every issue follows the same loop:

1. **Signal:** monitoring, weather, meter or imagery data identifies a problem.
2. **Diagnosis:** estimate affected capacity, urgency, probable cause and value at risk.
3. **Action:** route the work to the responsible party.
4. **Evidence:** capture photographs, readings, parts, serial numbers, cost and completion proof.
5. **Verification:** use operational data to confirm recovery and close the issue.

The long-term product is the asset-to-fault-to-action-to-recovery history across the portfolio.

## The USP

The defensible edge is not any single robot or dashboard. It is the combination of local operations, independent owner accountability and a compounding evidence dataset.

### Immediate differentiation

- Owner-side and vendor-neutral rather than tied to one inverter, EPC or O&M contractor.
- Measures action and recovery, not only alarms.
- Combines operational data with field evidence in one workflow.
- Uses specialist partners without forcing the owner to coordinate them.
- Recommends cleaning, mowing or inspection only when evidence supports the cost.
- Produces source-linked management and investor reporting as part of operations.

### New developments that strengthen the proposition

**AI operations copilot**

AI can normalise alarms across vendors, rank issues by likely lost generation, identify repeat faults, extract evidence from engineering reports and produce source-linked monthly reports. Human review remains mandatory for engineering conclusions and contractual reporting.

**Multimodal evidence graph**

SCADA readings, work orders, component records, drone imagery, photographs, warranties and post-repair performance are linked to the same asset and incident. This creates a reusable failure and resolution history that improves with every site.

**Evidence-triggered robotics**

Robotic cleaning and mowing are deployed only when the economics and site geometry support them. The company sells the result and uses the most suitable equipment or partner, avoiding dependence on one machine.

**Drone-to-work-order automation**

Thermal/RGB inspection findings become prioritised defects and work orders, rather than static PDF reports. Production data subsequently verifies whether each repair worked.

**Satellite site intelligence**

Planet imagery can screen broad vegetation growth, flooding, access change, construction and neighbouring land use. It is a triage tool that can reduce unnecessary visits, not a replacement for module-level thermal or electrical inspection.

**Cross-fleet predictive maintenance**

Once sufficient clean history exists, the company can identify component and serial-number cohorts with repeated faults, estimate likely maintenance demand and improve spare-parts planning. This is the potential long-term data moat, but it must be earned through operations rather than claimed at launch.

## What the company deliberately does not do initially

- operate a 24/7 control room;
- perform HV switching or direct plant control;
- guarantee plant availability;
- carry spare-parts or OEM warranty liability;
- manufacture proprietary robots;
- build autonomous rooftop installation systems;
- launch wind maintenance in parallel;
- build a large software platform before paid operating demand is proven.

## Commercial model

### Entry offer

A paid 60–90 day operational baseline and leakage audit:

- connect historical/read-only data;
- establish asset and performance baselines;
- identify unresolved losses and evidence gaps;
- produce an owner-ready operating pack;
- close at least one complete signal-to-action-to-verification loop.

Indicative test price: **€4,000–€8,000 per site**.

### Recurring service

Indicative managed operations fee:

- **€750 monthly portfolio base plus approximately €20 per MW per month**, subject to a minimum;
- specialist field work passed through transparently with a coordination and assurance fee;
- optional annual thermal, vegetation, cleaning and compliance modules.

Five 30 MW sites at the test formula would generate approximately **€81,000 ARR**. This proves the operating model but is not yet enough to support two full-time founders. The path to a meaningful company requires larger portfolios, higher-value assurance modules or UK expansion.

## Route to market

### First buyer

An independent Irish or UK owner, developer retaining assets, fund or investment vehicle with:

- multiple sites or more than approximately 20 MW;
- mixed portals, contractors or equipment;
- recurring manual reporting;
- no complete internal asset-management team;
- authority to grant read-only access;
- a measurable reporting, downtime, warranty or contractor-control problem.

### First 90 days

1. Clear employment, confidentiality, IP and conflict boundaries.
2. Use three to five warm renewable relationships for confidential problem interviews.
3. Review redacted monthly packs, alarms, contractor tickets and unresolved discrepancies.
4. Sell one paid baseline before building software or buying equipment.
5. Run the pilot manually with cleanly owned Evolv capabilities and specialist partners.
6. Measure owner hours removed, losses identified, response delays and evidence gaps.
7. Convert the pilot to a 12-month service.
8. Build reusable product components only after the same requirement appears across three customers.

## Expansion path

### Phase 1: Solar operating assurance

Monitoring, reconciliation, fault workflow, contractor evidence and reporting.

### Phase 2: Integrated field services

Drone thermography, cleaning, robotic mowing, drainage, security and environmental evidence through qualified partners.

### Phase 3: Portfolio intelligence

Cross-fleet fault benchmarking, component cohorts, predictive maintenance and insurer/lender reporting.

### Phase 4: Wind and storage

Add wind or battery reporting only for existing multi-technology customers. Continue to partner for specialist inspection and maintenance.

### Phase 5: Emerging construction robotics

Track utility-scale installation robots, heavy-lift roof logistics, flexible/adhesive PV and autonomous inspection. Become an Irish deployment or service partner only after a system has repeat commercial deployments, certification, warranty acceptance and support.

## The investment thesis

This begins as a high-trust managed service with limited capital requirements. Repeated operations produce the workflows, evidence and dataset from which a scalable software and intelligence layer can emerge.

The strongest strategic position is:

> The independent operating intelligence layer for renewable assets, connecting what the plant reports, what contractors do and what performance proves.

## Decision gate

Proceed only if one portfolio owner will pay for the operational baseline and provide read-only access. Do not buy a robot, drone or satellite subscription before that proof.
