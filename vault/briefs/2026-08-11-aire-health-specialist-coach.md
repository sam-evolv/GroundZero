# Aire Health specialist coach

## Summary

A future premium Aire experience, tentatively called **Aire Health** or **Aire Trainer**, that acts as a specialist health, fitness and wellbeing agent. It should feel like a dedicated room within Aire rather than a separate product: its own specialist `soul.md`, knowledge base, goals and routines, while accessing the user's approved general Aire context.

This is **parked for later revisit**, not an instruction to expand the current build now.

## Product shape

- User defines goals and relevant baseline information such as height, weight, training history, constraints and preferences.
- Coach mode: training plans, exercise substitutions, progression, sport-specific programmes and technique demonstrations.
- Nutrition guidance: calorie and protein targets, meal plans, recipes, pantry-aware substitutions, shopping lists and approval-gated grocery ordering.
- Recovery: sleep, fatigue, soreness, hydration, mobility, deloads and adaptations after travel, illness or poor sleep.
- Progress: trends across weight, measurements, workouts, endurance and strength, with optional progress photos and clearly expressed uncertainty.
- Reminders and nudges that turn a plan into weekly and daily action.
- One persistent loop: goal → programme → daily action → observed progress → adaptive plan → reduced friction.

## Strategic rationale

Most fitness products fragment training, food, recovery, reminders and tracking. Aire's potential advantage is continuity across the person's life: schedule, preferences, history, travel, constraints and approved personal context. A high-quality version could be a compelling top-tier subscription feature and a recurring relationship rather than a one-off AI answer.

## Boundaries and safety

- Treat this as health and wellness coaching, not diagnosis or medical care.
- Use “nutrition guidance” rather than “dietician” unless the necessary clinical and regulatory basis exists.
- Body-photo analysis should support progress tracking and coaching context, never claim precise body-fat measurement or medical interpretation.
- Health context should be private, inspectable, correctable and deletable, with explicit permission before general Aire accesses it.
- Sensitive measurements and photos are private by default and must never be used for advertising.
- Escalate potentially concerning symptoms rather than confidently coaching through them.
- Ordering, booking or purchasing remains approval-gated.

## Revisit questions

- Which initial wedge is strongest: strength, weight loss, running, general fitness or a specific sport?
- What evidence and expert review are needed before launch?
- Which health integrations are genuinely useful and privacy-safe?
- What can be delivered credibly without creating a medical or regulatory claim?
