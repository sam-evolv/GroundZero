# Consultancy quick-revenue strategy

## Goal

Generate near-term cash without derailing OpenHouse. The consultancy should sell fixed-scope AI/automation implementation packages to sectors with obvious admin pain, accessible decision-makers, and short sales cycles.

## Core principle

Do **not** sell generic AI consulting.

Sell:

> A fixed-fee implementation that removes a painful workflow within 7–14 days.

Best offers should be:

- understandable in one sentence
- tied to revenue, cost, time, or missed follow-up
- deliverable with existing tools
- reusable across similar customers
- able to produce a case study
- aligned with OpenHouse/OpenBook/Evolv insight rather than random agency work

## Best first sectors

### 1. Property managers, letting agents, estate agents and small developers

**Why first:** closest to OpenHouse, strong domain credibility, repetitive email/doc/photo workflows, owner/manager access is possible.

**Pain points:**

- tenant/homeowner maintenance requests
- photo-to-issue triage
- landlord/developer reporting
- inspection/snagging summaries
- viewing follow-up
- repetitive buyer/tenant questions
- document packs and handover materials

**Quick-win offer:**

> AI Maintenance & Follow-Up Desk: turn WhatsApp/email/photos into categorised issues, suggested replies, contractor briefs and weekly owner/client reports.

**Fixed-fee package:**

- €1,500 diagnostic + workflow map
- €3,500–€5,000 10-day implementation
- €500–€1,500/month support/hosting/iteration

**Why they buy:** fewer missed issues, faster replies, cleaner reporting, less admin, better client perception.

### 2. Renewable installers, retrofit firms, heat-pump/solar/EV companies

**Why first:** overlaps with Evolv and OpenHouse energy thesis, customers face repetitive aftercare/support, documentation and grant/admin workflows.

**Pain points:**

- customer education after install
- commissioning docs/manuals/warranties scattered
- repeated “how do I use this?” questions
- grant/SEAI paperwork and evidence packs
- quote follow-up and lead qualification
- maintenance reminder gaps

**Quick-win offer:**

> AI Aftercare Pack for installers: searchable customer handover pack, FAQ assistant, warranty/manual vault and automated follow-up sequence.

**Fixed-fee package:**

- €2,000 setup for one install type
- €4,000–€7,500 for docs + workflow + customer support assistant
- €750–€2,000/month ongoing support/content/reporting

**Why they buy:** fewer support calls, better customer experience, easier proof of handover, more professional aftercare.

### 3. Trades, contractors and small construction firms

**Why:** lots of money leaks through quoting, follow-up, job admin and document chaos; owner-led firms can decide quickly.

**Pain points:**

- quotes take too long
- enquiries get lost in WhatsApp/email
- RAMS/method statements/docs are repetitive
- invoice chasing
- job photos not organised
- handover packs not professional

**Quick-win offer:**

> Quote & Job Admin Copilot: intake form + quote draft + job folder + follow-up messages + invoice chase workflow.

**Fixed-fee package:**

- €1,500–€3,000 setup
- €300–€750/month support

**Why they buy:** faster quoting and fewer lost jobs. The ROI is immediate if it helps close even one extra job.

### 4. Clinics, dentists, physios, aesthetics and local appointment businesses

**Why:** overlaps with OpenBook; urgent no-show/follow-up/rebooking pain; small business decision-makers.

**Pain points:**

- missed bookings/no-shows
- poor follow-up
- manual reminders
- cancellation gaps
- FAQ/reception admin
- no review/rebooking loops

**Quick-win offer:**

> No-Show & Rebooking Automation Sprint: reminders, deposits/waitlist/rebooking prompts, missed-call capture and review requests.

**Fixed-fee package:**

- €750–€1,500 audit + setup
- €2,000–€4,000 implementation
- €250–€750/month support

**Why they buy:** no-shows directly cost money. This can also feed OpenBook.

### 5. Accountants, brokers and compliance-heavy professional services

**Why:** document chasing and recurring reporting are painful, but trust/compliance can slow sales. Good second wave, not first.

**Pain points:**

- client document collection
- repetitive client updates
- onboarding forms
- renewal reminders
- report drafting
- internal knowledge search

**Quick-win offer:**

> Client Document Chase System: AI-assisted intake checklist, reminder sequence, status dashboard and draft client updates.

**Fixed-fee package:**

- €3,000–€6,000 implementation
- €750–€1,500/month support

**Why second wave:** potentially high value, but more trust-sensitive and slower to close.

## Productised offers to sell

### Offer 1: AI Admin Audit + 10-Day Automation Sprint

**Promise:**

> In 10 days we identify one admin bottleneck and implement an AI-assisted workflow that saves staff time or recovers missed revenue.

**Price:**

- €500 paid audit, credited against build
- €3,500 implementation
- €750/month optimisation/support

**Deliverables:**

- workflow map
- inbox/form/intake process
- AI draft/reply/report workflow
- simple dashboard or tracker
- SOP and Loom training
- 30-day improvement plan

### Offer 2: Inbox/WhatsApp Triage Desk

**Promise:**

> Stop losing issues and enquiries in inboxes and WhatsApp.

Best for property managers, trades, installers.

**Deliverables:**

- enquiry/issue intake form
- category/severity tagging
- suggested replies
- contractor/customer brief generation
- weekly summary report

### Offer 3: Quote & Follow-Up Engine

**Promise:**

> Turn enquiries into quote drafts and follow-ups before leads go cold.

Best for trades, installers, consultants, local service businesses.

**Deliverables:**

- lead capture form
- quote information checklist
- quote draft templates
- follow-up sequence
- CRM/sheet status board

### Offer 4: Customer Handover / Aftercare Pack

**Promise:**

> Give every customer a professional AI-ready handover pack and reduce repetitive support.

Best for installers, developers, contractors.

**Deliverables:**

- document vault structure
- manuals/warranty register
- customer FAQ
- maintenance reminders
- post-install follow-up sequence

### Offer 5: No-Show & Rebooking Recovery Sprint

**Promise:**

> Reduce no-shows and recover cancelled appointment revenue.

Best for OpenBook-style venues.

**Deliverables:**

- reminder flow
- deposit/waitlist workflow if applicable
- cancellation recovery messages
- review/rebooking follow-up
- venue performance report

## Pricing ladder

### Starter: €500–€750 paid audit

- 60–90 minute workflow session
- systems/access review
- top 3 automations
- ROI estimate
- implementation quote

Use this to avoid unpaid consulting.

### Sprint: €2,500–€5,000 fixed implementation

- one workflow
- 7–14 days
- simple tools only
- handover docs/training

### Retainer: €500–€2,000/month

- monitoring
- iteration
- new templates
- staff support
- monthly report

### Premium: €7,500–€15,000

Only after case studies. Multi-workflow implementation or sector-specific package.

## What to avoid

- vague “AI transformation strategy”
- unpaid discovery calls longer than 30 minutes
- enterprise pilots with no paid scoping
- bespoke builds that cannot be reused
- heavily regulated workflows as first deals
- selling to committees
- promising full autonomy before trust exists
- work that has no OpenHouse/OpenBook/Evolv learning value

Enquiry comes in, the system collects missing info, drafts a quote/follow-up, and keeps a job tracker up to date. Fixed-fee setup, no big software migration. Worth a quick look?

It focuses on reminders, cancellation recovery, rebooking prompts and review requests. The aim is simple: recover a few missed appointments a month. Would you be open to a 20-minute audit?

## Best first wedge recommendation

Start with **property managers / estate agents / small developers** and **renewable installers**.

Reason:

- They align with OpenHouse/Evolv.
- Sam can speak credibly about their workflows.
- The pain is document/photo/email heavy.
- Implementations can become reusable product components.
- Case studies help the main OpenHouse story.
