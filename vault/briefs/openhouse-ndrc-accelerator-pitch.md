# OpenHouse NDRC accelerator pitch draft

## One-line positioning
OpenHouse gives every home a brain, starting with developer handover and aftercare, then expanding into homeowner support, energy intelligence, warranties, maintenance, and the persistent living home model.

## Current NDRC narrative

OpenHouse should not be pitched narrowly as a portal for developers. The NDRC version must be more ambitious:

> OpenHouse is building the AI operating layer for modern homes. It starts where home context is born, at handover, then turns documents, photos, issues, warranties, bills, and energy systems into a living home model that helps homeowners and property companies manage the home over time.

## Post-Cairn learning to include carefully

A major Irish developer effectively validated the category while declining to adopt externally. They acknowledged gaps around customer-care automation and energy/home intelligence, but internal inertia meant adoption was blocked.

Use this as learning, not grievance:

> We learned that the category is real, but founder-led sales into the largest developers is too slow and politically exposed before the company has outside legitimacy. That is why NDRC is the right next step: funding, network, investor discipline, and market access can convert a strong product into a fundable company with multiple routes to market.

## Core thesis
OpenHouse helps property developers prove quality, reduce friction, and strengthen the commercial story around each scheme by centralising the operational truth of handover and aftercare.

The company is not a generic portal or a broad AI company OS. It is a premium proptech system that sits on top of messy real-world delivery and turns it into defensible, customer-facing output.

## Why this matters now
- Developers are under pressure to deliver better buyer experience and cleaner proof.
- Handover and aftercare still create repeated admin, lost context, and avoidable friction.
- Compliance, sustainability, and buyer education are increasingly evidence-heavy.
- AI now makes it practical to compress the repetitive work without losing quality, if the system has strong context and guardrails.

## What OpenHouse does
- centralises scheme truth
- manages homeowner and developer communication
- tracks issues, snags, and aftercare
- surfaces missing or weak documents
- helps prove handover happened properly
- generates cleaner updates, summaries, and evidence packs

## 30-second pitch
OpenHouse is a premium proptech platform for property developers. It turns handover, aftercare, compliance evidence, and buyer communication into a structured system of record. Instead of scattered emails, PDFs, and spreadsheets, developers get one place to prove what happened, reduce friction, and manage the scheme properly.

## 2-minute founder pitch
OpenHouse is the premium evidence and workflow layer for property developers.

The problem is that handover and aftercare are still fragmented across emails, documents, spreadsheets, and support threads. That creates friction for developers, confusion for buyers, and weak proof when they need to show quality or compliance.

OpenHouse centralises the operational truth of a scheme. It helps teams manage homeowner communication, issue handling, document coverage, handover readiness, and aftercare in one place. The AI layer is there to compress repetitive work like summarising, classifying, and drafting updates, but the product stays premium and human-controlled where trust matters.

We are not trying to be a generic AI platform. We are building the system that developers can rely on when they need to prove handover quality and reduce post-completion friction.

## Likely investor or accelerator objections
### "Is this just a portal?"
No. The point is to become the operational evidence layer for the scheme.

### "Why not just use generic tools?"
Generic tools do not model the domain, the evidence, or the customer workflow tightly enough.

### "Where is the wedge?"
The wedge is proof-heavy handover and aftercare, especially where buyers, compliance, and operational clarity matter.

### "What is the moat?"
Trust, premium execution, structured scheme context, and workflow depth.
