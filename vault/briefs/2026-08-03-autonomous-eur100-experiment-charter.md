# Autonomous €100 experiment charter

## Challenge

Can Hermes produce at least €100 of new, stranger-funded economic value between Monday 3 August and Sunday 9 August 2026, with no ongoing labour from Sam?

## Honest success definition

To prevent the experiment from grading itself, success means:

- at least **€100 of customer money paid or irrevocably escrowed** for a completed or contractually accepted deliverable;
- net of marketplace fees, refunds and direct experiment spending;
- before income tax, which is handled separately by the legal merchant;
- no purchases by Sam, friends, family, Longview, OpenHouse or related parties;
- no subjective valuation of an unsold file, codebase or report;
- no prize merely entered, invoice merely sent, claimed time saving or affiliate commission not yet earned.

Cleared bank payout is tracked separately because legitimate platforms may hold funds beyond seven days.

## Irreducible human boundary

Hermes cannot legally own a bank account, complete KYC, be the merchant of record, pay tax or accept contractual liability. The accurate autonomy claim is **human-established, agent-operated, exception-supervised**. Fully autonomous commercial operations are possible only after one short setup by the human legal owner.

The minimum one-time setup is:

1. approve this charter and a hard spending ceiling;
2. supply or approve a separate experiment email and truthful seller identity;
3. complete any required KYC and connect an Irish payout account;
4. authorise Hermes to respond to explicit buyer requests, accept fixed-scope work within this charter, issue refunds up to money received and deliver work;
5. approve the exact disclosure describing agent-assisted production.

After that setup, Sam performs no research, selling, customer communication, delivery or project management.

If even that legal setup is prohibited, the experiment may create an asset but cannot honestly be said to have made euros.

## Active no-human lane

After the initial plan was written, research found an official agent-native rail rather than a human marketplace workaround. Superteam Earn publishes an official agent API that permits autonomous agents to register, discover listings marked `AGENT_ALLOWED` or `AGENT_ONLY`, submit work and later hand an award to a human claimant for KYC and payout.

Hermes registered the neutral agent `hermes-eur100-experiment` on 3 August 2026. The API key and claim code are stored outside Ground Zero in a mode-restricted Hermes secrets file and must never be committed or displayed.

The agent-only API returned nine visible listings, but all were expired and had winners announced. The ordinary feed returned 19 current listings, all marked `HUMAN_ONLY`. Therefore, there was **no live eligible bounty at activation** and no submission was made.

Cron job `d63b861b9802` now checks every two hours until the deadline. It may autonomously complete and submit only a future, explicitly agent-permitted, no-spend research/writing/data-analysis bounty that can be delivered without Sam's identity, social accounts, wallet, regulated advice, private data, prohibited scraping or external deployment. All other work fails closed. A separate one-shot job, `b43e228036dd`, reports strict PASS or FAIL at 23:59 on 9 August.

This lane needs no human operation before a win. If an award occurs, a human must still claim the agent, complete KYC and own the payout. Registration, submission or a displayed prize is not revenue.

## Proposed higher-probability sales lane

Do not build a speculative app, audience or passive-income product. Sell one high-value, agent-deliverable outcome to an existing buyer.

### Offer

**24-Hour Competitor Evidence Pack — €149 fixed**

The buyer supplies one product, market or decision. Hermes returns:

- five relevant competitors;
- current pricing, positioning and core claims;
- dated screenshots and exact source URLs;
- a machine-readable source ledger;
- a concise comparison of strengths, gaps and uncertainty;
- three decision-relevant findings;
- one revision;
- delivery within 24 hours of a complete brief.

No call is required. Public sources only. No legal, medical, investment or regulated advice. No private credentials or personal customer data.

### Why this route

- one sale can clear the €100 target after normal payment fees;
- existing buyers already request competitor and market research;
- the deliverable is evidence-verifiable rather than generic AI prose;
- Hermes can research, produce, quality-check and deliver it without Sam;
- no local outreach, public personal brand, OpenHouse material or daytime meeting is needed;
- it tests customer payment, not marketplace vanity metrics.

## Acquisition rule

Respond only to recent public buyer requests explicitly seeking market research, competitor analysis, source validation, product research or adjacent evidence work. Do not scrape contact lists, send mass unsolicited email, impersonate Sam, conceal AI use or contact OpenHouse/Longview relationships.

Before using any marketplace, verify that its current terms permit the account arrangement, AI-assisted delivery and proposed communication. Do not automate a platform that prohibits automation or account sharing.

Target:

- inspect at least 50 live buyer requests;
- qualify at most 20 where scope, budget and deadline fit;
- send a short response tailored to each request;
- quote €149 or the platform-currency equivalent;
- link one independently produced sample;
- state that the work is agent-assisted and evidence-checked;
- no meeting, retainer or custom software promise.

This is response to declared demand, not cold outreach.

## Seven-day operation

### Day 1

- verify payment, marketplace and disclosure terms;
- create a neutral experiment identity that does not imply a separate legal person;
- produce one public-data sample pack unrelated to Sam's businesses;
- create fixed scope, refund rule and payment/escrow route;
- build the buyer-request monitor.

### Days 2–3

- inspect and rank new buyer requests;
- respond to the best ten to fifteen;
- answer messages autonomously within the fixed scope;
- reject calls, sensitive data, regulated advice and ambiguous contracts.

### Day 4 gate

- if fewer than two genuine buyer conversations exist after ten qualified responses, rewrite positioning once;
- introduce a smaller **€69 Rapid Snapshot** only where the buyer's stated budget cannot support the full pack;
- require two snapshot sales to meet the target;
- do not add features, buy ads or start another product.

### Days 4–7

- continue to a maximum of 20 qualified responses;
- accept only work deliverable within 24 hours;
- research with primary/public sources;
- run an independent evidence and hallucination audit before delivery;
- deliver PDF plus source ledger;
- handle one revision and payment confirmation;
- record gross amount, fees, direct costs, refund exposure and net result.

## Budget and authority ceiling

Proposed ceiling: **€30 total**, including proposal credits, model/API costs and payment fees not deducted from revenue.

Hermes may not:

- open credit, borrow, trade, gamble or buy cryptocurrency;
- purchase ads, followers, reviews or customer lists;
- accept recurring subscriptions;
- enter obligations above €250;
- use Sam's employer, OpenHouse or Longview identity or evidence;
- process sensitive personal data;
- provide regulated advice;
- accept work requiring deception, spam, fake accounts, prohibited scraping or IP infringement;
- subcontract to undisclosed humans;
- continue after the seven-day deadline without new approval.

## Stop conditions

Stop immediately if:

- platform terms do not permit agent-assisted operation;
- KYC or account ownership would require deception;
- a customer requests sensitive/private data or regulated advice;
- direct spend reaches €30;
- the work cannot be independently verified;
- a refund or dispute would reduce net revenue below zero.

At Sunday 23:59, report PASS or FAIL without redefining value. A failed sale experiment is still useful evidence about autonomous distribution; it must not be disguised as €100 of created value.

## Why Poe, Metaculus and human-only public bounties are not the primary sales route

Poe is cheap to test but typical organic creator earnings are unverified, and a new bot may receive no distribution. Metaculus has real funded prize pools but outcome resolution and prize payment do not fit a strict one-week cash test.

A live check of Superteam Earn's ordinary feed on 3 August found 19 open listings, including several $250–$1,000 research or content bounties due within the week. Every one was marked `HUMAN_ONLY`, so the neutral agent will not enter them. The separate official agent feed is now monitored as described above. Public open-source bounties were independently rejected because of heavy competition, rejected submissions and poor net returns after model costs.

## Payout timing and liability tail

The seven-day metric is net revenue booked, with cleared payout reported separately. New-account payouts commonly exceed the experiment window: Stripe says an initial payout is typically scheduled 7–14 days after the first successful payment; Lemon Squeezy holds net sales for 13 days before its payout schedule; Gumroad applies at least a seven-day holding period and may review the account.

Real customer money also creates a liability tail after day seven. Refunds, chargebacks, data requests, IP complaints and tax records do not vanish when the experiment ends. If the human owner wants no ongoing operational role, the safe response to an exceptional event is to refund where appropriate, suspend sales, preserve records and issue one concise exception notice.
