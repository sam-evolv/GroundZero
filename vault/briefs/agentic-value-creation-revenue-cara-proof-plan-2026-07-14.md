# Agentic Value Creation, Revenue and Cara Proof Plan

## Executive call

Do not add more generic tools, MCPs, dashboards, model providers, permanent agent swarms, or autonomous outbound workflows now.

The stack is already capable enough. The missing loop is:

> evidence → decision → approved action → measured outcome

The first commercial loop should be the existing **AI Maintenance & Follow-Up Desk** outreach test. The offer, first Cork property list and seven-day success gate already exist. The next value-creating action is to send the first 10 approved messages, not to build another system around the messages.

The second loop should be an OpenBook retention intervention queue, but only after the acquisition test is either validated or killed.

Cara remains the primary product bet. Its next proof is not wider telephony or generic SME automation. It is a reliably useful founder voice loop, then one narrow paid inbound-call workflow for one service-business vertical.

## Value creation rule

Judge every AI addition by one question:

> Does it create booked revenue, protect recurring revenue, remove recurring delivery cost, create decisive customer proof, or materially improve an approved decision?

If not, park it.

## Priority 1: Founder sales desk

### Existing evidence

- [[items/consultancy-cork-property-outreach]] already contains a specific offer and first 10 Cork property prospects.
- The current gate is: 10 messages, 2 meaningful replies, 1 call, and 1 paid €500 audit within seven days.
- No external outreach should be sent autonomously. Hermes prepares research, evidence-led openers, follow-up dates and call briefs; Sam approves and sends.

### Outcome ledger

Record every contact as: drafted, approved, sent, delivered, reply, call, offer, paid audit, objection or no response.

At the end of the seven days:

- **Pass:** productise the offer and repeat with a refined segment.
- **Miss:** change the offer or segment. Do not blame the model and do not add new tooling.

## Priority 2: OpenBook retention intervention queue

### Why it matters

OpenBook project state records 362 live venues and names no-shows as the top churn driver. Retention is revenue protection, but this needs a read-only evidence trial before any scoring platform or automated contact.

### Four-week proof

1. Select 25 venues with deterministic risk signals: no-show trend, booking decline, response delay, support friction or cancellation pattern.
2. Prepare a short venue-specific intervention brief and proposed human-approved action.
3. Run for four weeks against a comparable non-contacted cohort.
4. Measure engagement, reactivation, retention and false positives.

Only keep intervention types that show a measurable lift. Do not build a broad health-score system first.

## Priority 3: Evaluation and approval layer

Every agentic workflow that can harm trust needs a versioned evaluation set, shadow mode, human review and a release gate.

### OpenHouse

Prioritise real failure cases involving warranty relevance, urgency routing, unsupported claims, tenant/scheme contamination and appropriate escalation.

### Cara

Measure post-call artefact usefulness, cross-call memory correctness, interruption recovery, invented facts or commitments, transcript/action accuracy and human rescue rate.

Do not grant automatic external actions until a predefined quality threshold and override rate are met.

## Priority 4: Evolv reporting worker

Keep this deterministic: source export → schema validation → normalised report → exception report → human sign-off → immutable audit copy.

No model reasoning in calculations and no autonomous filing. The first test is reproducing one historical pack in parallel and requiring exact agreement on totals, dates, units and fields.

## Cara: the actual durable product

Do not sell Cara as an autonomous SME employee or a generic AI receptionist.

The durable wedge is an **Irish service-SME inbound revenue desk**:

> Cara turns every suitable inbound call into a qualified lead, booked callback, or clean human handoff.

The generic realtime voice layer will commoditise. Cara’s durable value is the vertical operating layer: Irish business vocabulary, Eircodes, service areas, local phone expectations, owner-approved policies, structured call outcomes, integrations, evaluation data and a trustworthy audit record.

### Initial action set

- capture the lead;
- establish job type and location;
- check deterministic rules such as hours or service area;
- request or tentatively arrange a callback;
- take a message;
- transfer or escalate;
- send the owner a structured receipt.

Do not add payment-taking, exact quotes, safety-critical advice, autonomous dispatch, aggressive outbound calling or broad CRM permissions.

### 30-day proof sequence

**Days 1–3:** choose one founder-dogfood call class. Write the policy, fields, safe actions, escalation rules, forbidden claims, baseline and launch gates.

**Days 4–10:** make the full founder loop reliable: inbound call → qualification → structured capture → safe action or handoff → owner receipt → outcome label. Test interruption, background noise, poor signal, Irish names, Eircodes, voicemail, silence, caller correction and tool failure.

**Days 11–17:** create and run a 100-call evaluation set: 40 normal calls, 20 noisy/ambiguous calls, 15 unsupported asks, 15 escalation scenarios and 10 adversarial or policy-boundary cases. Fix the highest-frequency failure class and rerun the full suite.

**Days 18–24:** run the narrow workflow with real calls and visible human fallback. Review every call daily. Recruit one paid Irish design partner, not a free broad pilot.

**Days 25–30:** produce a one-page proof report: eligible calls, answer rate, correct lead capture, callbacks/bookings, rescue rate, errors/complaints, recovered opportunity, owner minutes saved and cost per handled call. Scale, narrow or stop based on that evidence.

## What not to do now

- more MCP servers or model providers;
- re-enabling n8n without a repeated workflow that needs it;
- autonomous email, DMs, booking, payments, price changes, production changes or data mutation;
- permanent specialist-bot swarms or more dashboards;
- broad RAG/wiki expansion without a current decision use;
- Cara telephony/platform expansion before founder dogfooding meets its quality bar.
