# OpenHouse DTC acquisition hooks research

## Executive take

The strongest DTC wedge is **not “AI home assistant”**. It is a paid, event-triggered **Home Rescue / Home Operating System setup** that turns a messy house purchase or confusing bill into a concrete plan:

> Upload your inspection report, utility bill, appliance photos and closing docs. In 15 minutes get your prioritized first-year home plan, savings opportunities, warranty/inventory vault, and reminders.

Best near-term hooks are pain/event based:
- new move-in overwhelm
- first scary utility bill
- heat pump / solar / EV bill confusion
- renovation / retrofit planning
- insurance inventory
- warranty / receipt chaos

## Evidence signals found

- Homeowner repair anxiety is real: 2024/2025 search results surfaced surveys reporting 83% of homeowners encountered unexpected home repairs and many spent $5k+; separate new-buyer data claims 72% spent up to $10k on unexpected repairs in first two years.
- Insurance inventory has a clear authority-backed job: the Insurance Information Institute says an up-to-date home inventory helps settle claims faster, verify losses, and buy the right insurance.
- Energy complexity is rising: DOE maintains consumer pages for heat pumps and home energy rebates; search results show recurrent aux/emergency heat questions and high-bill confusion.
- Solar/EV homes have a math/control problem: Irish search results show active confusion around EV tariffs, solar export, batteries, time-of-use pricing and optimization.
- Renovation/retrofit intent is large and trust-constrained; grants and contractor choices add complexity.
- Warranty/service-contract space has buyer demand but low trust. This is an opening for an independent “know what’s covered / what to do next” vault rather than selling warranties first.

## Ranked buyer personas and hooks

### 1. New homeowner in first 90 days — highest priority

- **Moment:** offer accepted, inspection received, closing, moving week, first weekend in house.
- **Emotional trigger:** “I just made the biggest purchase of my life and don’t know what breaks first.”
- **Job:** translate inspection, seller disclosures, appliance labels, utility setup, warranties, safety checks and maintenance into a prioritized first-year plan.
- **Promise:** “Turn your new house into a manageable home in 15 minutes.”
- **Wow feature:** upload inspection PDF/photos → AI extracts issues, ranks by urgency/cost/safety, creates 30/90/365-day checklist, identifies shutoffs/filter sizes/model numbers, starts warranty/inventory vault.
- **Offer:** $49–$99 one-time Move-In Home Plan; upsell $9–$15/month monitoring/reminders.

### 2. First scary utility bill / energy detective — very strong wedge

- **Moment:** first winter/summer bill, post-move bill shock, new smart meter, new tariff.
- **Emotional trigger:** “Something is wrong and I’m being overcharged.”
- **Job:** explain bill, detect likely causes, compare tariff, map usage to equipment and behaviour.
- **Promise:** “Find why your home bill jumped — and what to do this week.”
- **Wow feature:** upload utility bill + photos of thermostat/HVAC/water heater/meter → diagnosis: tariff issue, estimated base load, heat pump aux heat, immersion/water heating, EV charging, insulation, billing estimate vs actual.
- **Offer:** $19 bill diagnosis or $79 annual bill watchdog; affiliate/lead fee for tariff switch or energy monitor.

### 3. Heat pump owner / retrofit confusion — strong specialist niche

- **Moment:** first cold snap after heat pump install, quote comparison, grant application, aux/emergency heat shows on thermostat.
- **Emotional trigger:** “Was this expensive upgrade a mistake?”
- **Job:** make heat pumps understandable, prevent expensive settings, document installer/warranty, troubleshoot high bills without becoming HVAC expert.
- **Promise:** “Make your heat pump cheaper and less confusing.”
- **Wow feature:** thermostat screenshot + bill + model label → plain-English operation guide, settings checklist, questions for installer, maintenance schedule, grant/warranty docs vault.
- **Offer:** $29 heat-pump checkup; partner with installers/retrofit advisors.

### 4. Solar + EV + battery household — high ARPU, niche but motivated

- **Moment:** after solar install, buying EV charger, moving to smart tariff, noticing export/import oddities.
- **Emotional trigger:** “I spent thousands and still don’t know if I’m optimizing it.”
- **Job:** explain tariffs, export credits, overnight charging, battery strategy, ROI and anomalies.
- **Promise:** “Stop guessing when to charge, export, or use your solar.”
- **Wow feature:** bill + inverter/charger screenshots → optimized daily schedule and tariff comparison.
- **Offer:** $49 optimization report + $8/month alerts; partnerships with solar/EV installers.

### 5. Renovation / retrofit planner — high value, longer cycle

- **Moment:** bought older home, planning kitchen/extension/BER upgrade, grant/loan process.
- **Emotional trigger:** “I don’t know what order to do things or who to trust.”
- **Job:** sequence projects, compare quotes, understand grants, avoid rework, maintain home record.
- **Promise:** “Know what to fix first before spending €20k+.”
- **Wow feature:** inspection + photos + goals → phased renovation roadmap: safety/moisture/energy/cosmetic, grant checklist, quote questions.
- **Offer:** $99–$299 planning report; later contractor marketplace/lead fees.
- **Caution:** riskier as first wedge because purchase cycle is episodic and scope can balloon.

### 6. Insurance inventory / disaster preparedness — evergreen, credible, lower urgency

- **Moment:** renewing insurance, moving in, buying high-value items, after local flood/fire/storm news.
- **Emotional trigger:** “If something happened, I couldn’t prove what I owned.”
- **Job:** create inventory fast; attach receipts/photos/model numbers; store safely; estimate coverage gaps.
- **Promise:** “Build a claim-ready home inventory in one afternoon.”
- **Wow feature:** video/photo walkthrough → itemized room inventory with estimated categories, receipts, serial numbers, policy checklist.
- **Offer:** free/low-cost lead magnet; bundle into Home Vault subscription.
- **Caution:** people know they should do it but procrastinate unless tied to move-in or insurance renewal.

### 7. Warranty / appliance vault — useful retention feature, weak standalone wedge

- **Moment:** appliance breaks, new appliances delivered, closing docs received.
- **Emotional trigger:** “I don’t know if this is covered or who to call.”
- **Job:** register/store warranties, receipts, model numbers, service history; advise claim path.
- **Promise:** “Never lose a receipt, model number, or warranty again.”
- **Offer:** bundle, not primary acquisition. Could be a free scanner that feeds subscription.

## Recommended wedge sequence

### Wedge 1: Move-In Home Plan

Primary audience: first-time/recent buyers and people moving into a new house.

Landing headline:

> **Your house came with 200 pages of documents. We turn them into a first-year action plan.**

Subpromise:

> Upload your inspection report, utility bill and appliance photos. Get a prioritized 30/90/365-day plan, hidden-cost warnings, maintenance reminders, warranty vault and claim-ready inventory.

Why first:
- highest emotional urgency
- broad appeal
- easiest activation artifact: inspection/report/docs
- naturally seeds every later feature

### Wedge 2: Bill Shock / Home Energy Detective

Retarget move-in users when first bills arrive; standalone SEO/search wedge around “why is my electric bill so high,” “heat pump aux heat,” “EV tariff,” etc.

Landing headline:

> **Your bill is high. Find out if it’s the tariff, thermostat, water heater, EV, or the house.**

### Wedge 3: Heat Pump + Solar/EV Specialist Packs

Vertical calculators/checkups for high-complexity homes.

Headline:

> **Make your heat pump/solar/EV setup pay back faster — without spreadsheets.**

### Wedge 4: Renovation / Retrofit Roadmap

Upsell after inspection/action-plan output identifies bigger projects.

Headline:

> **Before you renovate, know the right order.**

### Wedge 5: Vault subscription

Retention layer: inventory, warranties, appliance docs, service history, maintenance calendar, contractor notes, insurance packet.

## Product packaging and pricing

- Free: Home sanity check — upload one bill or 5 appliance photos; get limited findings and checklist.
- $49–$99 one-time: Move-In Home Plan.
- $19–$29 one-time: Utility bill / heat pump checkup.
- $49–$79 one-time: Solar/EV tariff optimization.
- $99–$299: Renovation/retrofit roadmap.
- $9–$15/month or $79–$129/year: Home Vault + reminders + seasonal checkups + bill/watch alerts.
- B2B2C: estate agents, mortgage brokers, insurers, utilities, installers can subsidize a branded Move-In Plan.

## Landing page promise

Hero:

> **The AI home agent for your first year in a new home.**

Alternative sharper hero:

> **Know what to fix, what to ignore, and what’s costing you money.**

CTA:
- Upload your inspection report
- Diagnose my first bill

Outcome bullets:
1. Prioritized 30/90/365-day home plan.
2. Utility-bill and tariff red flags.
3. Appliance, warranty and manual vault.
4. Claim-ready photo inventory.
5. Seasonal reminders before small issues become expensive.

Avoid generic claims:

> Ask anything about your home.

Use concrete before/after:

> Before: 200-page survey + random notes. After: 12 urgent actions, 18 reminders, 6 documents saved, 3 likely savings opportunities.

## Acquisition channels

- SEO/search: “new homeowner checklist”, “what to do after buying a house”, “why is my electricity bill so high”, “heat pump aux heat expensive”, “home inventory insurance template”, “appliance warranty lookup”, “solar EV tariff Ireland”.
- Agent/mortgage broker closing gift: $49 Move-In Plan voucher.
- Utility/installer partnerships: heat pump/EV/solar onboarding companion.
- Paid social: new movers, first-time buyers, homeowners with EV/solar/heat pump interests.
- Local content: Ireland/UK grants, BER/retrofit, smart meter tariffs, SEAI/utility-specific guides.

## Validation tests

1. Smoke-test landing pages for three headlines: Move-In Plan vs Bill Shock vs Heat Pump Checkup.
2. Concierge MVP: manually process 20 inspection reports/bills into plans within 24h; charge $49. Track perceived value, refund rate, top repeated outputs.
3. Agent channel test: ask 10 estate agents/mortgage brokers to offer as closing gift; measure recipient activation.
4. Bill diagnosis test: run ads/search pages for “why is my electric bill so high”; charge $19; manually analyze bills to learn patterns.
5. Inventory scanner test: ask users to video one room; return inventory PDF; test whether they upgrade to Vault.
6. Heat pump cohort: recruit from forums/Facebook/installer customers during winter; offer $29 checkup; measure savings claims and testimonials.
7. Pricing ladder test: compare $49 one-time plan, $99 premium plan with live review, and $9/month vault attach.

## Key recommendation

Lead with **new-home move-in + first-year operating plan**, then quickly attach **bill shock diagnostics** as the second monetizable moment.

This gives OpenHouse a concrete, urgent consumer reason to exist now, while building the longitudinal home memory needed for the broader AI home agent vision.
