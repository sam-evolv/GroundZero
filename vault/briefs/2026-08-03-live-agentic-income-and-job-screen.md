# Live agentic-income and job-switch screen

## Outcome

Four live official listings survived. Only one is a verified evening-compatible side-income route available in Ireland. It is useful as a cash experiment, not an escape plan.

The remaining three are full-time job-switch candidates. They are not compatible with being run quietly beside Sam's current job after hire.

## 1. Outlier: English writing and content reviewing for AI training

**Action:** Apply as a bounded income-bridge test.

- Official listing: https://app.outlier.ai/en/expert/opportunities/4613691005
- Engagement: freelance/1099 contract, not employment.
- Eligibility: the live listing explicitly includes Ireland in `allowedCountries`.
- Schedule: fully remote and flexible; contributor chooses when and how much to work.
- Pay: up to USD $15 per hour for core project work. Onboarding and some non-core work may pay less. Weekly payouts are stated.
- Live evidence: official Outlier job endpoint returned the listing on 3 August 2026; 24,169 contributors were shown as hired that month.
- Fit: strong writing, evaluation, AI-tool use and careful reasoning align directly with Sam's demonstrated work.
- Limits: task availability and hours are not guaranteed; the rate is a modest cash bridge, not high-leverage monetisation.

A ten-hour week at the maximum core rate would be USD $150 before tax, fees and unavailable-task time. Do not budget against it until actual paid tasks are available.

## 2. Remote: Product Manager, Contractor Management

**Action:** Apply if Sam is willing to consider a serious full-time job switch.

- Official listing: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/remotecom/jobs/7817061003
- Engagement: permanent full-time employee.
- Location: Remote-EMEA; Ireland is within the stated EMEA scope, subject to ordinary right-to-work checks.
- Pay: published global annual range USD $50,000 to $126,000, location-adjusted. This is not an Irish salary promise.
- Live evidence: official Greenhouse record returned HTTP 200 and was updated 29 July 2026.
- Fit: Sam has built products end to end, works asynchronously with distributed agent workflows and understands contractor/business operations.
- Gap: the listing asks for prior product-manager experience, ideally in a high-growth company. Founder product ownership is relevant evidence but not a substitute the employer must accept.

- Live evidence: official Greenhouse record returned HTTP 200 and was updated 21 July 2026.
