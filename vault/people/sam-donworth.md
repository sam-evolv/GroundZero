- Sam's 2022 UCC Department of Economics honours dissertation was titled "An investigation on the relationship between the Cryptocurrency market and the Stock market throughout the COVID-19 global pandemic." It used OLS regression and parameter-stability tests across five international stock markets and two cryptocurrencies and was selected for inclusion in University College Cork's Boole Library. Do not describe it as peer-reviewed or journal-published.
