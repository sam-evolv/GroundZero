# Goal: Prove Aire as an AI-operated company

## Target

Within six months, establish Aire as a real customer product and produce independently reviewable evidence that AI safely operates most repeatable company work under explicit founder oversight.

## Proof gates

### Product proof

- Real customers use Aire for recurring administrative work.
- Completed work and Time Given Back are measured conservatively.
- Retention and repeat delegation show the product is useful beyond a demonstration.

### Operating-system proof

- 100 percent of recurring company workflows in scope are documented with trigger, owner, inputs, outputs, evidence, approval boundary, failure mode and rollback.
- Each automated workflow has an eval and an audit trail.
- Consequential actions remain approval-gated unless a written decision changes the boundary.
- Company operations record AI completion, human intervention, failure, recovery and cost.

### Rehearsal proof

- Complete two controlled founder-absence rehearsals lasting two to three weeks each.
- Accumulate at least 30 days of reliable operation before public launch.
- No unresolved critical security, financial, legal or customer-impact incident.
- Independent review can reproduce the evidence and identify every human intervention.

### Distribution proof

- Prepare one falsifiable public experiment and sanitised live operating view.
- Build a source-backed Irish media and podcast route only after the evidence exists.
- Position Sam through practical proof of automation, not generic AI commentary.
- Route attention back to Aire's promise of removing unwanted administration and returning time.

## Non-goals

- Declaring full autonomy before the evidence exists.
- Replacing product traction with a publicity stunt.
- Buying hardware before workload, reliability and economics justify it.
- Moving into generic consultancy before Aire earns product proof.
- Removing founder accountability or consequential approval boundaries.
