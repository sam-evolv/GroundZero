# Health Index

Private health and fitness tracking for Sam.

## Active block

- [[2026-07-21-recomposition-block]]

## Operating principle

Run one measured block at a time. The goal is not perfect compliance or scale obsession. The goal is enough accurate data to make one useful adjustment at a time.

## Privacy

Physique photographs and dietary/health information are sensitive. Store only images Sam chooses to share, avoid face imagery unless useful, and never share or publish health data without explicit consent.
