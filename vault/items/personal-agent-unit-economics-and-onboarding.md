# IrelandGPT unit economics and onboarding validation

## Outcome

Produce an evidence-backed onboarding, trial, pricing and cost model showing how IrelandGPT can let people experience value before signup while keeping every incremental paid customer contribution-positive.

## Acceptance criteria

- Current model names, modalities and API prices are verified from primary sources.
- A provider-agnostic workload taxonomy covers text, reasoning, coding, vision, documents, voice, image, video, tools, memory and retries.
- Founder dogfood captures cost per completed outcome across materially different tasks.
- Irish/EU gross prices are converted to net revenue after VAT and payment fees.
- Infrastructure, storage, bandwidth, observability, auth, notifications, support, fraud and refunds are included.
- Guest abuse controls and an explicit maximum acquisition-cost allowance are modelled.
- Median, heavy, power and adversarial usage scenarios are modelled.
- €9.99, €19.99, premium and family hypotheses are compared without assuming they are viable.
- Trial structures compare bounded guest use, time-limited trial and paid-first conversion.
- Signup supports Apple, Google and email with measured step count and completion.
- The living Profile has inspect, correct, forget, provenance and export requirements.
- The optional context conversation is tested for completion and downstream usefulness.
- Family billing is separated from child surveillance.
- No child monitoring or parent-alert feature proceeds without specialist Irish/EU privacy, safeguarding and clinical review.
- Final output states contribution margin, break-even users, tier allowances and the evidence confidence for each assumption.

## Evidence workstreams

1. Model and tool pricing: initial primary-source ledger complete; price-change monitoring and evaluations remain.
2. Irish consumer usage: CSO adoption and subscription-readiness baseline complete; IrelandGPT willingness-to-pay evidence remains.
3. Guest conversion and signup friction: external evidence complete; product experiment remains.
4. Payment, VAT and failure economics: public Stripe/VAT baseline complete; actual account pricing, refunds and fraud remain.
5. Context-profile value and trust: product design and user evidence remain.
6. Family privacy, safeguarding and account isolation: initial legal/product research complete; specialist counsel and impact assessments remain.
7. Founder workload telemetry: not started.
