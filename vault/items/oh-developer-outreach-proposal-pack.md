## What this is
A reusable template that converts a warm developer introduction into a tailored discovery-plus-proposal artefact quickly. A short discovery checklist to run the first call, then a one-page proposal shell that plugs in the developer's scheme count, timeline, and the OpenHouse proof assets. Content leverage, not a build.

## Opportunity size
Direct and near-term. Bridgewater is verified as a high-fit, five-scheme Cork prospect and is the live test case. If warm intros keep arriving through Carol Tallon and other channels, the bottleneck shifts from lead generation to conversion speed and consistency. A repeatable pack removes per-deal rework and makes each conversation land more professionally, which is exactly what an early developer sale needs.

## Technical approach
- Write a short discovery checklist covering scheme pipeline, current handover and aftercare process, pain, and decision path.
- Build a one-page proposal shell: problem framing, what OpenHouse does, the Longview reference, scope for a first scheme, and clear next step.
- Wire it to draw from [[items/oh-proof-asset-engine]] so proof stays consistent and current.
- Dogfood it on the Bridgewater conversation, then refine from what actually converts.

## Risks
- Over-templating can make proposals feel generic; the pack must force a real tailoring step per developer.
- Do not promise capabilities the market-readiness audit shows are not yet rollout-ready; scope first engagements to what is truly live.
- Low value if warm-intro flow does not materialise, so keep effort small until Bridgewater proves the shape.

## Effort
S. A checklist and a one-page shell reusing existing assets. The refinement comes from real conversations, not up-front build.

## Market timing
Timely. The Bridgewater introduction is being prepared now, so the pack is most useful in the immediate window before that first developer proposal is needed.
