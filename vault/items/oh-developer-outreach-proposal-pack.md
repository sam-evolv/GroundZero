## Material proposal, 21 August 2026 — approval-gated paid-validation queue

### Bottleneck

[[project_state/oh]] now records paid developer validation as the explicit commercial gate associated with Enterprise Ireland funding, while the existing founder-sales system still requires a repeated manual cycle across a 31-row pipeline: recheck the current buyer and route, decide which account is due, recover the relevant proof and prior contact state, draft the next message, set the follow-up date and update the scorecard. [[briefs/2026-08-05-openhouse-founder-sales-operating-kit]] already assigns this preparation work to agents so Sam does not spend each evening on list research, and records a target of 20 qualified first contacts per week plus separate follow-ups. The current state still names 24 active contact-ready accounts and ten first-wave plus two second-wave drafts awaiting approval. This is a verified recurring preparation bottleneck, not evidence that more outbound volume alone will create a paid pilot.

This proposal extends the existing discovery-to-proposal pack; it does not create a second sales strategy, revive Bridgewater, Cairn, Glenveagh or O'Flynn, or automate relationship judgement.

### Value category

- **Near-term revenue and funding evidence:** keeps the paid-pilot validation gate in front of Sam without opening more product work.
- **Founder time reclaimed:** removes repeated account-state reconstruction, role checking, follow-up-date calculation and draft assembly.
- **Decision quality:** makes every send decision show the source, relationship route, last action, due date, proof used and exact commercial ask.
- **Relationship and claim risk reduction:** keeps stale roles, closed routes, unsupported proof and generic high-volume messaging out of the approval queue.

### Smallest live test

Run one read-only, draft-only weekly queue over **five** currently contact-ready accounts already named in the corrected Ireland, UK or US lanes. For each account:

1. load its existing Ground Zero record, current pipeline stage and prior draft;
2. re-verify the operating owner's current role and the company's active scheme or workflow from a live source;
3. classify the route as `WARM`, `DIRECT`, `CHANNEL`, `STALE` or `CLOSED` without inventing an introduction;
4. prepare exactly one next action: first message, follow-up one, follow-up two, discovery brief, proposal input request or close/park recommendation;
5. attach the supporting source URLs, last-contact evidence, due date, approved Longview proof boundary and any missing field;
6. emit a compact approval queue and a deterministic scorecard delta.

The test may use [[briefs/2026-08-05-openhouse-first-message-approval-queue]], [[briefs/2026-08-05-openhouse-founder-sales-operating-kit]] and [[briefs/openhouse-longview-developer-proof-pack-2026-08-03]] as controlled inputs. It must not send, connect, comment, schedule, update an external CRM or create a proposal commitment.

### Evidence of success

- All five rows have a live-verified buyer role, route, company/scheme hook, pipeline stage, last action, due date, exact next action and source links; unknown fields remain `UNKNOWN`.
- No closed or materially downgraded route is presented as active without new evidence.
- Every draft stays within the approved Longview evidence boundary and asks for one bounded workflow conversation or paid-pilot next step rather than implying enterprise readiness.
- Sam can approve, edit, park or reject all five rows in under ten minutes, with no account-state research needed during review.
- A second unchanged run creates no duplicate draft or alert; a reply, role change, elapsed follow-up threshold or explicit correction changes only the affected row.
- After four weekly runs, the scorecard can distinguish targeting, reply, meeting, qualification, proposal and payment bottlenecks using the existing decision rules; preparation volume is not counted as commercial success.

### Downside and failure mode

Role data and company hooks can go stale, inferred relationship routes can damage trust, and a polished queue can encourage low-signal volume or hide that the wedge is not converting. Follow-up timing may also be inappropriate after a personal introduction, explicit rejection or active conversation. Fail closed on unclear identity or prior-contact state, cap the queue at five for the test, preserve Sam's direct corrections, and stop if drafts remain generic or review time is not materially lower. The system diagnoses the funnel; it does not manufacture buyer intent.

### Approval boundary

Automation may read the named Ground Zero sales notes and public company or role sources, draft and rank five next actions, calculate follow-up dates and prepare a proposed scorecard delta. It may not send email or LinkedIn messages, make connection requests, submit contact forms, book meetings, contact introducers, update an external CRM, publish proof, quote a price, issue a proposal, promise scope, spend money or change production. Sam approves every external contact, wording, recipient, price, proposal and commitment. A live role check does not authorise outreach.

### What it replaces

It replaces the repeated manual preparation loop of reopening the 31-row pipeline, checking whether a buyer and route are still current, reconstructing prior context, drafting the next message, calculating the follow-up date and updating the weekly scorecard. It does **not** replace founder-led relationship judgement, Sam's network use, discovery calls, the commercial ask, [[items/oh-proof-asset-engine]], this proposal pack, [[items/oh-marketing-site-conversion-instrumentation]], or the existing UK and US validation sprints.
