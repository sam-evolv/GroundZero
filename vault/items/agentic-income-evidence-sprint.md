# Agentic income evidence sprint

## The thesis

The monetisable capability is not "Hermes can work for hours". Buyers do not pay for invisible effort. They pay for a high-stakes outcome that would otherwise be slow, risky or expensive.

The strongest candidate offer from Sam's demonstrated work is a fixed-scope, asynchronous **Evidence and Release Audit** for founder-led AI or B2B products.

## Offer

> In 72 hours, receive an evidence-grounded audit of your live product, website and launch claims, with the trust breaks, unsupported claims, release risks and highest-conversion fixes ranked in one decision-ready pack.

## Initial deliverable

- Live product and marketing-site review against the buyer's stated audience and goal.
- Claim ledger separating verified fact, supplied claim, inference and unsupported assertion.
- Exact top five trust or conversion breaks with screenshots or source evidence.
- Rewritten hero, proof order and primary CTA where needed.
- Release gate covering broken links, mobile rendering, critical accessibility, exposed secrets and obvious deployment errors.
- Decision-ready PDF and one revision.

No custom software build, ongoing consulting retainer, cold call or daytime meeting is included.

## Why Sam can credibly deliver it

- He has shipped and independently verified product, marketing and release work across OpenHouse and David Howard Golf.
- He has built a conservative six-page developer proof pack from live evidence.
- His working method already includes exact-artifact review, claim boundaries, mobile QA, security checks and live-domain verification.
- The work is naturally asynchronous and can be completed outside his full-time role.

These are internal credentials. Any public sample must remove private company, homeowner, employer and credential information and must not imply third-party endorsement.

## Unconventional marketplace pivot

Sam rejected the low-paid platform and job-switch routes as insufficiently compelling. [[briefs/2026-08-03-unconventional-agent-income-research]] now records the stronger hypothesis: publish a narrow pay-per-result machine capability into an existing automation marketplace rather than sell hours or build another standalone SaaS. The first bounded candidate is a more reliable Google Trends Apify Actor, selected from current official marketplace demand, failure and rating data. It remains a no-spend falsification test, not an income claim.

1. Produce one anonymised sample page from existing permitted material.
2. Publish one fixed offer at an introductory price of **€250 for the first three audits**. This price tests purchase, not long-term economics.
3. Apply only to current buyer-posted briefs or platforms that explicitly accept Irish evening/async contractors.
4. Cap effort at ten tailored applications or one listing plus ten days of observation.
5. Do no local cold outreach and do not promote it through Sam's personal LinkedIn.
6. Stop if there is no qualified response after the cap. Do not turn weak demand into another website or SaaS build.

## Success and kill criteria

**Success:** one paid audit from a stranger, or two qualified buyer calls/messages from active briefs.

**Kill:** no qualified response after ten credible shots, or buyers consistently require daytime consulting, deep engineering credentials or bespoke implementation.

## Candid constraint

Distribution is the risk. Agent throughput improves delivery economics but does not create demand. This is a bounded test, not evidence that an immediate side-income business exists.

The faster financial route may still be a materially better full-time role. The live opportunity screen should rank both paths rather than protecting this idea.
