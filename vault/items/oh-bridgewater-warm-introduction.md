# Convert Carol Tallon’s Bridgewater Developments warm introduction

## Opportunity

Carol Tallon, who previously hosted Sam on iProperty Radio, asked whether OpenHouse is still taking on developers. She believes Bridgewater Developments may be interested and offered to introduce Sam.

This is a **warm lead**, not part of the later scaled cold-outbound campaign. It should be handled now while the marketing site and outbound system are refined in parallel.

## Working rule

Do not delay an inbound, qualified introduction until the marketing website is perfect. Do not use this as permission for premature mass outreach either.

The right sequence is:

1. Accept Carol’s introduction promptly.
2. Research Bridgewater’s live developments, buyer experience, and likely decision-maker context.
3. Tighten only the landing-page proof and message needed to survive the inevitable “let me look at your website” moment.
4. Make the first conversation about Bridgewater’s handover, aftercare, homeowner evidence, and buyer-experience friction, not a generic product demo.
5. Send a tailored follow-up only after the discovery conversation.

## Ready-to-send reply to Carol

> Hi Carol, thanks for thinking of OpenHouse. Yes, absolutely, I would really appreciate an introduction to Bridgewater. We are focused on helping developers create a much clearer homeowner handover and aftercare experience, with the evidence and home context kept in one place rather than lost across documents, emails and support threads. I would love to learn how Bridgewater currently approaches that side of the customer journey and see whether there could be a useful fit. Thanks again, Sam

**Status:** draft only. Do not send without Sam’s approval.

## Prospect research

A verification pass is in progress. Do not assume development count, scale, decision-makers, or commercial fit until the source-linked research is back.

## Success condition

A scheduled discovery conversation with the appropriate Bridgewater decision-maker, after Carol’s warm introduction, with a sharp public landing page and a tailored discovery brief ready.
