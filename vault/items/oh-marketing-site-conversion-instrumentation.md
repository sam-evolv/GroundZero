# Marketing site v3 conversion instrumentation

## Why now
Marketing site v3 ships as part of the committed 24 July focus, immediately followed by investor and developer outreach. Every outreach email and award application will point traffic at this site. If it launches without instrumentation and a single strong call to action, Sam cannot tell which outreach messages or channels convert, and the outreach cycle runs blind.

## What
Ship with the site, not after it:
- One primary CTA everywhere: "See a live scheme" (book a 20-minute walkthrough of the real Longview deployment). A live-data demo is the strongest asset OpenHouse has and most proptech competitors demo empty sandboxes.
- Lightweight analytics (Vercel Analytics or Plausible, one afternoon): page views, CTA clicks, source/UTM. Tag every outreach email, award profile, and LinkedIn link with a distinct UTM so replies and visits map back to the message that caused them.
- A simple booking link (Cal.com or Calendly free tier) behind the CTA so a warm developer can self-book without an email round trip.

## Why it fits the focus decision
This is finishing the committed marketing-site work properly, not a new build. Cost is under half a day and it converts the outreach cycle from unmeasurable to measurable.

## Success
- Site live with CTA, analytics, and booking link before the first batch of investor/developer emails goes out.
- After two weeks: know visits, CTA click rate, and bookings per outreach channel.
