## Opportunity size
The current manual reporting pain is small in absolute hours, but it exposes a bigger product angle. A client portal can turn compliance output into a reason for rooftops to stay engaged and recommend the service.

## Technical approach
- Build on top of the grid export automation.
- Publish monthly generation, export, and compliance evidence in a simple portal.
- Add PDF and CSV exports for owners, installers, and auditors.
- Keep the first version read-only and tightly scoped.
- Use the same data pipeline for operations and sales proof.

## Risks
- Portal scope can sprawl before the core automation is stable.
- If the data refresh is unreliable, the portal loses trust fast.
- There may not be enough live sites yet to justify a broad product build.

## Effort
M. Could start as a lightweight reporting page and grow into a fuller portal later.

## Market timing
Good. Distributed energy operators increasingly need simple evidence, reporting, and stakeholder visibility without hiring more admin help.

Treat as the productized follow-on to the automation work. If the first automation lands cleanly, this becomes a credible upsell and differentiation angle.
