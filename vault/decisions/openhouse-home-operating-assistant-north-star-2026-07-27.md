# OpenHouse home operating assistant north star

## Decision

OpenHouse's DTC category north star is the home-specific operating assistant:

> **OpenHouse knows your home and helps you run it right.**

The internal platform remains a living Home Record, evidence graph, event engine, value ledger and approval-controlled action layer. The consumer product is the assistant, not the architecture.

## First recurring value thesis

Energy and homeowner operation are the first recurring proof loop. OpenHouse should help the household understand how this specific home is intended to perform, identify meaningful deviations, recommend a bounded action, and verify the outcome where evidence permits.

OpenHouse must not blame homeowners for operating complex systems incorrectly. The consumer framing is:

> Modern homes are complicated. OpenHouse helps you get the best from yours.

## Long-term distribution decision

OpenHouse should be designed so its permissioned Home Record can later serve other assistants through a scoped MCP or equivalent context interface. General assistants can become interfaces. OpenHouse remains the trusted authority for this home's facts, evidence, systems, permissions and history.

This is a future architecture requirement, not a current integration claim.

## Consequences

- The product must optimise for home-specific intervention, not generic chat volume.
- A guided spatial and systems capture is now a priority feasibility experiment, not a committed product promise.
- The event-engine pattern becomes a core product primitive: observe, infer, qualify, notify, explain, recommend, verify and remember.
- Energy claims must separate measured change, comparable-period change, estimated cost difference and verified saving.
- Household behaviour data cannot be treated as a hidden asset for sale.
- The €79 Upgrade Ready validation remains active, but it must now be tested against a Home Performance Check proposition before the winning acquisition wedge is fixed.
- Developer handover remains the best route to a verified day-one Home Record and therefore strengthens this DTC north star.

## Not decided

- whether Upgrade Ready or Home Performance wins acquisition
- whether walkthrough capture can meet the accuracy and friction bar
- which recurring Irish data source supports broad monitoring
- whether households will pay for an ongoing assistant
- whether any aggregate research product is appropriate
- when a permissioned MCP interface should be built
