# Personal agent delegation-first product promise

## Decision

IrelandGPT will be designed and positioned as a personal operating system that removes unwanted responsibility through reliable delegation. It will not be designed or marketed as another AI chatbot, model wrapper or integration catalogue.

The primary product test is whether a user can hand over a hated job and receive a completed outcome, artifact or clear receipt with minimal thought.

## Why

Ordinary users care whether the job was completed, not which model, provider, connector or specialist agent produced it. The emotional value is time returned and the confidence that the person does not need to think about the task again.

## Consequences

- Conversation remains the front door, but durable handoffs, Doing, Done and You become the primary product structure.
- The first-use experience must complete a bounded, high-friction job within minutes rather than explain capabilities.
- Connectors are requested just in time and in context, never presented as technical setup inventory.
- Model selection, routing and specialist capabilities remain invisible during ordinary use.
- Sending, booking, buying, deleting, account changes and sensitive sharing remain explicitly approval-gated.
- Personal context is inspectable, confidence-aware, correctable and revocable.
- Irish administrative jobs will be researched and ranked before the product claims a broad consumer wedge.
- Pricing near €5, €15 and a professional tier remains a hypothesis until usage, cost and support evidence exists.
- Household and child features remain future work with explicit private/shared boundaries and separate safety research.
- Founder marketing uses verified completed work as proof, and Sam approves every outward post.

## Relationship to the runtime-first decision

This decision complements [[decisions/2026-08-06-personal-agent-runtime-first-sequence]]. Hermes remains the authoritative engine and capability breadth is preserved. The delegation-first promise governs how that breadth is shaped, explained and validated for ordinary users.
