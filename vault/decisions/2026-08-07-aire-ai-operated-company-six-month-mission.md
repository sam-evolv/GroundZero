# Make Aire an evidence-proven AI-operated company

## Decision

For the next six months, Aire will pursue two linked outcomes:

1. Build a consumer personal agent that automates the administrative junk people hate and returns time.
2. Use the same system to progressively automate Aire's own software-company operations, creating a credible public proof that establishes Sam as Ireland's leading practical AI and automation founder.

The internal operating model becomes product proof. The public demonstration happens only after repeated private rehearsals and at least one month of auditable evidence.

## Why

Aire is fundamentally an agentic automation company. It cannot credibly sell time-returning automation while operating its own repeatable work through avoidable manual administration.

Ireland's smaller AI ecosystem creates a chance to establish category authority earlier than would be possible in a more crowded market. The authority must come from a difficult, verifiable execution rather than commentary or inflated technical positioning.

## Consequences

- Customer value remains the first gate. The autonomy story cannot substitute for a product people use.
- Every recurring company process needs a workflow, evidence, eval, failure mode, approval boundary and owner.
- Founder ownership and oversight remain explicit.
- Financial, legal, security, customer promises and irreversible publication remain human-controlled until narrowly defined evidence supports any change.
- The public proof must disclose human interventions and excluded functions.
- "Fully autonomous" is not an approved claim in advance. The final language must match the audited operating reality.
- The likely public experiment is a two-week founder absence after two rehearsals and one month of reliable private operation.
- Consulting and automation inbound are strategic upside, not the immediate business model or a reason to divert from Aire.
- Specific future models or local hardware are enablers, not dependencies in the plan.

## Success definition

By the end of the six-month horizon, Aire should have:

- real customers receiving measurable time back;
- an internal company workflow map;
- a growing set of supervised and bounded autonomous operations;
- auditable completion, intervention, failure and recovery records;
- repeated private founder-absence rehearsals;
- an independently reviewable public proof package;
- a truthful claim strong enough to earn Irish media and founder attention.
