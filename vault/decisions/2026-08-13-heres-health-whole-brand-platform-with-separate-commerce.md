# Propose a whole-brand Here’s Health platform while keeping Square and Shopify commerce separate

## Decision

Sam will position the engagement as **Here’s Health Digital Platform, Phase One**, using the new café as the launch event and high-frequency use case while including existing Shopify retail as immediate customer value.

In version one:

- Square remains operational and transactional source of truth for café commerce.
- Shopify remains operational and transactional source of truth for retail commerce.
- Café and retail products will not share one basket or transaction.
- The customer-facing application may unify navigation, brand, preferences, favourites, notifications and filtered history.
- Cross-system rewards and broader AI scope will not delay launch.
- Final contracted scope remains gated on Week 1 discovery and technical proof.

## Why

- A café-only app has limited value before the new café opens.
- Shopify gives the app immediate utility and an installed-channel opportunity before launch.
- Café ordering creates a higher-frequency reason to retain the app after launch.
- Combining Square and Shopify transactions would add payment, refund, tax, inventory and fulfilment risk without equivalent Phase One customer value.
- The expanded proposition supports premium commercial positioning and a longer-term digital-transformation relationship.

## Consequences

- The proposal must price and describe a platform, not a café app with free retail extras.
- Week 1 must prove both commerce paths and the staff fulfilment workflow before the six-week scope is locked.
- The design must feel unified while clearly distinguishing Café and Shop.
- The launch plan needs a protected cut line for rewards, advanced personalisation, content depth, sauna integration and AI.
- Here’s Health must own the Apple and Google organisation accounts and clarify ongoing support ownership.
