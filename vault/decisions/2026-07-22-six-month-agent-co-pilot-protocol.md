# Decision: Run a private-by-default six-month agent co-pilot experiment

## Commitment and explicit non-choice

Sam will use Hermes and Ground Zero as a structured external brain for six months, starting with a 14-day proof phase.

The non-choice is a public AI-transformation announcement, a new content business, unrestricted life logging, or handing decision authority to an AI. The system exists to improve human-led execution, not to create a public performance or replace judgement.

## Objective and metrics

Improve the quality and speed of decisions across:

- body composition, training, sleep, and energy;
- day-job execution;
- cash clarity and evidence-backed bridge activity;
- one commercial/founder proof loop at a time;
- durable context capture and reduced re-litigation.

Primary proof is changed behaviour and externally grounded outcomes, not note volume, streaks, AI usage, or claimed motivation.

For the first six-week cycle, limit active outcomes to health adherence/recomposition and cash authority. Day-job performance, relationships, and OpenHouse remain guardrails, not parallel optimisation projects.

## Assumptions and falsifiers

- Assumption: short structured capture will reduce cognitive friction and improve follow-through. Falsifier: by 5 August it produces more admin/pressure without changed commitments or outcomes.
- Assumption: private evidence before public sharing will create a more credible founder narrative. Falsifier: no meaningful evidence artefacts or practical lessons emerge after the proof phase.
- Assumption: one explicit cash/proof loop plus health and job discipline is sustainable beside employment. Falsifier: sleep, job performance, training, or relationships deteriorate.

## Alternatives considered

1. Start a public Substack immediately. Rejected for now because it creates an audience obligation before proof and may become performance instead of execution.
2. Continue ad hoc voice notes without a formal protocol. Rejected because context is then captured but not measured or reviewed.
3. Let AI rank and direct all priorities. Rejected because it would create dependency and remove the human judgment the system is meant to improve.

## Pre-mortem

1. **The system becomes productivity theatre.** Early signal: many notes and no customer, job, body, or cash outcome. Mitigation: 10-minute daily cap, one active proof loop, weekly kill test.
2. **It becomes emotionally over-dependent.** Early signal: Sam seeks AI permission for ordinary personal decisions or avoids human support. Mitigation: retain approval boundaries, use human relationships/professionals where needed, minimise capture rather than increase it.
3. **It fragments attention across all ventures.** Early signal: work begins on OpenHouse, OpenBook, Cara, Evolv, content, and cash bridge in one week. Mitigation: WIP limit, one founder/commercial proof loop, explicit parked list.
4. **It turns into public performance before results.** Early signal: Substack/LinkedIn content is planned before two weeks of private evidence. Mitigation: defer public decision to 5 August review.

## Review trigger

Review on 5 August 2026. Continue, simplify, or stop based on observable outcomes and whether the system changed decisions or calendar blocks.
