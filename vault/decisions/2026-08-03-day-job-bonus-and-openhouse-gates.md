# Keep the day job through the January bonus unless an exit gate is met

## Decision

The default is to remain in the current job until the January bonus is paid. Sam should not resign merely because the current period feels stagnant.

This is a working financial decision, not a commitment to remain indefinitely.

## Why

- The bonus is close enough that walking away before it is paid would destroy real value without creating an equivalent commercial gain.
- OpenHouse does not yet provide dependable founder income.
- Waiting preserves runway and negotiating power while warm developer conversations and accelerator outcomes develop.
- The emotional desire to work for himself is legitimate, but it does not make an unfunded resignation financially sound.

## Exit gates that override the default

Leave earlier only if one of these becomes real and independently verified:

1. **Accelerator gate:** YC or another credible funded accelerator accepts OpenHouse on terms that allow Sam to work full-time.
2. **Commercial gate:** contracted OpenHouse revenue, committed investment or a combination provides a credible personal runway after tax and company costs.
3. **Job-switch gate:** a materially better role is formally offered and improves income, learning or founder runway enough to justify the move.
4. **Health or integrity gate:** remaining becomes genuinely unsafe or requires conduct Sam cannot accept. This is not the current reported state.

## Not an exit gate

- A bad weekend.
- Feeling behind.
- An accelerator rejection.
- Silence from one developer or investor.
- More product code without a buyer.

## Review rhythm

Review only when a gate changes or once monthly. Do not relitigate resignation every difficult morning.
