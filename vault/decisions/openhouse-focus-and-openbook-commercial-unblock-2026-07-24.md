# OpenHouse focus and OpenBook commercial unblock

## Decision
Keep the current experiment focused on OpenHouse. Do not divert into the previously discussed alternative plan. In parallel, spend only a controlled, commercially justified amount of weekend time unblocking OpenBook's existing customer revenue.

## Why
- Sam's evening capacity is constrained by full-time work.
- OpenHouse is the chosen path toward investment or revenue and its marketing site is close to finished.
- OpenBook already has a customer and a second interested prospect, but the promised client-edit portal blocks charging.
- A €79/month subscription becomes meaningful quickly against approximately €300/month of AI and development subscriptions.

## Consequences
- OpenHouse marketing-site completion and investor/developer outreach come first.
- OpenBook work is limited to the smallest usable self-edit portal, Stripe billing, and a narrowly scoped lead-gen automation proof.
- Do not return to manually building ten bespoke demo sites per day.
- Instagram outreach remains a manual copy-paste action, with automation preparing the lead, link and message rather than impersonating Sam.
