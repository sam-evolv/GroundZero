# Decision: Cara is the primary founder bet; self-serve follows founder proof

## Decision
Sam will focus his discretionary founder energy on making [[companies/cara|Cara]] genuinely excellent rather than spinning up a separate near-term consultancy cash engine.

The intended business is not a high-touch service. The target is a self-serve, plug-and-play digital assistant that a customer can sign up for online and immediately use, once reliability and onboarding meet a high bar.

## Why
- Cara has materially larger upside than a fragmented services business.
- It can make a concrete difference to people’s daily lives: an always-available, warm assistant that remembers context and takes real actions.
- It is intellectually and technically demanding in a way Sam wants: voice, trust, memory, actions, onboarding and reliability must all work together.
- Founder dogfooding is a uniquely strong product-quality loop: Sam’s own long, nuanced, hands-free conversations reveal the standards the product must meet before broad distribution.

## Guardrails
- This is not permission to rush into a generic self-serve landing page or broad public launch.
- First prove the founder voice loop: repeated 20–60 minute conversations with natural latency, patient endpointing, barge-in, continuity and review artefacts.
- Then prove a narrow action pack and an onboarding path that can activate without Sam personally implementing every account.
- A self-serve product is the destination; founder dogfood and narrow early pilots are the route.
- Avoid creating side ventures that dilute this focus. Near-term cash pressure remains real, so decisions about employment must remain financially deliberate rather than emotionally reactive.

## Consequences
- [[items/cara-founder-voice-dogfood]] is the top product milestone.
- Cara’s public positioning should evolve from an Irish SME assistant toward a general callable assistant only as the underlying voice/action loop proves reliable.
- The AI personal brand can be built from genuine product lessons and proof, not as an independent content treadmill.
