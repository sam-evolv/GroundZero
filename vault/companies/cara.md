# Cara

Cara is Sam's new Irish SME venture idea now being built: a phone-number-with-a-brain for small businesses. Customers ring it and get a warm always-on digital assistant; owners ring or message it as a PA that books jobs, drafts paperwork with approval, runs phone errands, and remembers the business.

## Positioning

- Core pitch: **hire your first employee for about €3/day** — she answers the phone, does paperwork, chases follow-ups, and never sleeps.
- Emotional wedge: small business owners are short of rest; Cara lets the business keep working while the owner stops.
- Ireland/EU-first: +353 flow, Irish voices/culture, EU data residency, GDPR, Art. 50 compliance, Grow Digital Voucher story.
- Strategic moat: callable number + business memory + actions + local trust + pack flywheel.

## Non-negotiables

- User-facing product language says **digital assistant**, not AI/bot/agent/model/automation, except statutory call disclosure.
- Money actions always require explicit owner approval before sending or charging.
- Every instruction becomes a visible persistent task; no silent stalls.
- No mid-conversation memory lapse; memory gauntlet is a hard acceptance path.
- EU residency and DPAs for call data.

## Current build focus

Phase 0: prove one excellent live +353 call before building app/dashboard/payment surfaces.

1. M1 — One live call: Twilio + Deepgram + Cartesia + Claude + Supabase transcript.
2. M2 — Ingestion: business name to structured business profile JSON.
3. M3 — Irish accent + memory gauntlet.
4. M4 — Ring-back continuity.
5. M5 — Trades pack + Google Calendar booking + owner SMS/WhatsApp summaries.
