# Here’s Health

## Snapshot

Established Cork family business with multiple health-food stores, cafés, an online Shopify store and a sauna offering. A new café is expected to open in approximately three months. Sam was introduced to owner Conor by Keith Crowley.

## Current engagement

- Original client request: a Square-connected click-and-collect app for the new café.
- Sam’s proposed expansion: **Here’s Health Digital Platform, Phase One**, a unified premium mobile product spanning Café, Shop, Rewards, Home and Account.
- Internal delivery target: production-ready candidate within six weeks, followed by approximately two weeks for review, release preparation and App Store dependencies.
- Current stage: awaiting Conor’s information and secure access to Shopify and Square before scope can be finalised.
- Reported but not independently verified: Square has told Conor the integration is possible.

## Product boundary

- Square remains source of truth and transaction system for café catalogue, ordering, payment and fulfilment.
- Shopify remains source of truth and transaction system for retail catalogue, inventory, basket, checkout and fulfilment.
- Café and retail baskets stay separate in version one.
- A unified customer-facing brand, profile, favourites area, history and notifications may sit above the two systems.
- Cross-system rewards unification is later-stage work and must not delay launch.
- The app and store listings should be owned through Here’s Health organisation accounts, not Sam’s personal accounts.

## Strategic value

- The existing Shopify business can make the app useful before the café opens.
- The café can provide a high-frequency retention loop and launch event.
- The project can become a flagship EvolvAI case study and open a longer-term AI and digital-transformation relationship.
- Conor has expressed interest in discussing operational AI after returning from holiday at the end of August.

## Open questions

- Has Here’s Health accepted the expanded platform scope, or only the original café pilot?
- What budget and commercial model are approved?
- What Square account, location, hardware, catalogue, loyalty and fulfilment setup exists?
- What Shopify plan, account model, checkout configuration and API access exist?
- What is the exact café opening date?
- Who owns operational acceptance, content, legal policies, app accounts and ongoing maintenance?
